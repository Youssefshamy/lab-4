# Repair the GPA, then transfer to UH-GAF

The best evidence-backed destination for this student is **the University of Hertfordshire's branch campus hosted by the Global Academic Foundation (UH-GAF) in Egypt's New Administrative Capital**, studying for a **BEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering**. The safest way in is a staged plan: spend one semester at NGU lifting the cumulative GPA to at least 2.0, collect written credit evaluations in parallel, then transfer. No university anywhere in the research meets all five priorities on published evidence. None publishes a rule that accepts D grades for engineering credit from a student below a 2.0 GPA and also leads to a degree that is clearly recognised in Egypt. The places that are lenient on paper (Northern Cyprus, US online schools) fail on recognition. The places that are clearly recognised (Egyptian private universities, Malaysia, UAE, Turkey) block a 1.8–1.9 GPA or D grades outright. UH-GAF and its close rival **TKH-Coventry** sit in the middle:

- Both explicitly accept transfer students.
- Both are UK awards with a 40% module pass mark, resits and coursework-heavy assessment.
- Both report Egyptian Engineers Syndicate recognition.
- Students live at home, with no visa and no SCU "study-abroad" risk.

How many of the ~84 NGU credits will count is **unknown at both until they issue a written evaluation**. Realistically, expect **2–3 years remaining**, at roughly **EGP 0.65–1.2 million in tuition at UH-GAF**. That is not much faster than finishing at NGU, which is why the evaluation step and the GPA repair come before any commitment. The single most important missing fact is how NGU converts a D into a percentage, because Egypt's transfer rules reportedly require at least 60% in every transferred course.

**How the evidence was gathered.** Nearly every source was read through search-engine extracts, because direct page fetches to university and news domains were blocked by the research environment's network proxy. Every figure below is attributed to the URL the extract came from, but the full pages were not read. **Re-verify each fee, threshold and deadline on the live page, or in writing with the institution, before acting.** Transfer estimates carry one of four labels:

- "Confirmed by an official individual credit evaluation" (none exists yet for this student);
- "Supported by published university policy";
- "Preliminary estimate based on course matching";
- "Unknown and requiring written confirmation".

## 1. Best overall: UH-GAF's Hertfordshire BEng in EEE, reached in two stages

**Recommendation: University of Hertfordshire at GAF (UH-GAF), New Administrative Capital, Egypt — BEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering.** The degree is awarded by the University of Hertfordshire, and the campus operates as an international branch campus under Egypt's Law 162/2018 ([Herts GAF page](https://herts.ac.uk/international/global-study-opportunities/transnational-education-partnerships/global-academic-foundation)). A BEng Mechanical Engineering & Mechatronics is the fallback specialisation if it preserves more credit ([GAF Mechatronics admission](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/admission/beng-mechanical-engineering-mechatronics)).

The case rests on how it scores against each priority, in the student's order.

**Priority 1 (easiest courses and graduation).** UK undergraduate modules typically pass at 40% ([OpenEduCat UK gradebook](https://openeducat.org/gradebook/uk/)). Hertfordshire classifies honours from the best 90 credits at Level 6 (weighted 75%) and the best remaining 90 credits at Level 5 (weighted 25%). The university itself says this "forgives a 'slip-up'" ([Herts honours classification](https://herts.ac.uk/study/how-to-apply/honours-classification-bachelors-degree)). It also means NGU grades and any credit transferred in never enter the final classification. GAF's scholarship page refers to "referral/retake students", which confirms that a resit route exists ([GAF EEE scholarship](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/scolarship/beng-electrical-and-electronic-engineering)).

The honest caveat is that this is not a soft degree. Engineering Council rules for accredited programmes allow no condonement of modules that deliver AHEP outcomes. They cap compensation at 30 credits with a 30% floor, and they forbid compensating the major project ([EngC policy](https://engc.org.uk/media/pujh1e3q/compensation-and-condonement-policy-2021.pdf)). Weak maths-heavy modules cannot simply be averaged away. That Herts' classification rules apply unchanged at the GAF campus is an inference: GAF students follow the same learning outcomes and receive the same UK award ([Herts GAF page](https://herts.ac.uk/international/global-study-opportunities/transnational-education-partnerships/global-academic-foundation)).

**Priority 2 (maximum credit, including D grades).** GAF publishes a **"transfer fee from another university" of EGP 10,000 / £250** ([GAF fees](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/tuition-fees)), and it asks transfer applicants for an official transcript and stamped course content ([GAF how to apply](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/how-to-apply)). Transferring in is therefore a routine, priced route, not an exception. GAF does **not** publish a minimum grade, a credit cap or an advanced-entry level for transfers. Its FAQ asks whether transfer students must take the foundation year, but the answer could not be retrieved ([GAF FAQ](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/faq)). Admission eligibility and credit approval are separate questions, and both remain **"Unknown and requiring written confirmation"**.

**Priority 3 (time and cost).** The programme runs as an International Foundation Programme (IFP) plus three years. Published 2025/26 engineering tuition is **EGP 310,000–370,000 per year** depending on category, with rises of up to 10% a year ([GAF fees](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/tuition-fees); [GAF EEE programme](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/programmes/beng-electrical-and-electronic-engineering)). The student lives at home and pays no visa, travel or blocked-account costs. That removes £20,000+ of UK living costs, or AUD 29,710 a year in Australia (sources in Section 5).

**Priority 4 (flexible admission below 2.0).** No GPA floor is published. Whether Egypt's national inter-university transfer rules apply to a Law-162 branch campus is unknown. Those rules require a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0, at least 60% in each transferred course, and at least 3 remaining years for programmes longer than 4 years ([Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818); [Ahram Gate](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2950190.aspx)). This is the main reason for the staged plan below.

**Priority 5 (recognised degree).** GAF reports that its programmes are "officially equalised by the Ministry of Higher Education" ([GAF equalisation news](https://gaf.edu.eg/news/our-programmes-are-officially-equalised-by-the-ministry-of-higher-education-and-scientific-research)). It also reports recognition by the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate ([GAF Syndicate news](https://gaf.edu.eg/news/uh-gafs-recognition-by-the-egyptian-engineers-syndicate)). Both claims come from the institution's own news pages. The Syndicate registers only Egyptian engineering degrees or degrees the Supreme Council of Universities (SCU) certifies as equivalent ([Nazra Q&A on Syndicate law](https://nazra.org/sites/nazra/files/attachments/questions_and_answers_engineers_syndicate_law_en.pdf)). A branch-campus degree that already holds equalisation avoids the case-by-case, after-graduation SCU review that every degree earned abroad faces ([SCU equivalency administration](https://scu.eg/en/administrations/general-administration-for-academic-degree-equivalency/)).

**Why UH-GAF edges out TKH-Coventry.** The two are nearly tied, and TKH-Coventry actually has the **stronger recognition paper trail**:

- SCU Decree 151 (29 May 2023) equated its BEng degrees for 240-ECTS completers ([TKH](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-degree-equivalency-egypt/); [Elwatan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/6631799)).
- Syndicate Decree 3/2024 approved its programmes, and its graduates have registered ([TKH](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-university-branch-engineering-graduates-officially-registered-at-the-egyptian-engineering-syndicate/)).

UH-GAF wins on the student's higher-ranked priorities for three reasons:

- Its engineering prices are published in EGP and map clearly to engineering. TKH's GBP fee table was garbled in extraction.
- The awarding university documents a forgiving classification rule and runs a formal transfer/APL process into Years 2 and 3 on its UK campus ([Herts transfer](https://www.herts.ac.uk/study/how-to-apply/transfer-to-herts)).
- No requirement was found to hand in a university withdrawal letter at application. TKH requires one ([TKH admission](https://tkh.edu.eg/admissions-coventry/)), which would force the student to give up their NGU place before seeing an offer.

**If TKH's written evaluation grants more credit than UH-GAF's, choose TKH-Coventry.**

**The staged plan.**

*Stage 0 (now to December 2026).* Obtain the NGU documents in Section 10. Send the three emails in Section 9. Submit a written pre-enrolment inquiry to the SCU equivalency portal ([equ.scu.eg](https://equ.scu.eg/GuideLines.aspx)).

*Stage 1 (Spring 2027 at NGU).* Use the current and next NGU semesters to lift the cumulative GPA to at least 2.0. Do this mainly by repeating D and F courses, if NGU's repeat policy replaces grades. NGU's repeat policy could not be found and must be confirmed. A GPA of 2.0 does several things:

- It removes the most likely admission objection at both branch campuses.
- It satisfies the reported national transfer floor ([Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818)).
- It is the published minimum at UND, Youngstown State and EMU ([UND](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/apply.html); [YSU](https://catalog.ysu.edu/undergraduate/general-information/admission/international-undergraduate-admission/); [EMU](https://registrar.emu.edu.tr/en/students/transfers)).
- It is required to graduate from essentially every Egyptian credit-hour programme ([NMU evaluation system](https://www.nmu.edu.eg/en/students/evaluation-system)).

*Stage 2 (intake after the repair semester).* Enrol at UH-GAF (or TKH) only after receiving a **written** statement of the entry level and the modules credited. Intake dates were not found and must be confirmed.

**The honest counterweight: finishing at NGU.** If both branch campuses credit less than about one year of NGU work, finishing CCAS at NGU after the GPA repair may be just as fast and cheaper. NGU's 2023/24 engineering fee was EGP 152,250 plus USD 750 a year ([NGU fees](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-news-events/newgiza-university-ngu-announces-the-minimum-admissions-requirements-for-its-schools-and-its-tuition-fees/)). The degree is domestic and SCU-recognised, although the CCAS specialisation title's Syndicate status is unconfirmed. The trade-off is that NGU's courses are the ones producing D grades. Moving to UK-style assessment is the main lever on priority 1.

The **two Egyptian branch campuses** lead because they combine a UK pass/resit culture with documented Egyptian recognition and no relocation costs. **Hertfordshire's UK campus** ranks third because it is the same award with the most explicit published APL process. Its Year 2 entry could also be the shortest route of all, at 4 semesters. The price is about three times UH-GAF's, and it carries the unresolved risk that the SCU will treat a 3-year UK BEng as short of its 240-ECTS / 4-year expectation. That expectation appears in the Coventry-branch decree, where 3-year completers were offered extra courses ([TKH](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-degree-equivalency-egypt/)).

**Debrecen** is the best non-UK option because it is the only university found that **commits in writing to which courses it will validate before the student transfers** ([UniDeb Faculty of Engineering transfer](https://eng.unideb.hu/node/286)). Its fees are moderate ([UniDeb fees](https://www.edu.unideb.hu/p/tuition-fee-application-entrance-fee)). Its Mathematics and Physics entrance exams work against priority 1 ([UniDeb EE BSc](https://edu.unideb.hu/p/electrical-engineering-bsc)).

**UND on campus** is fifth. It has the most generous published D-grade policy of any recognised-format engineering programme, but it only opens after the GPA repair ([UND transfer requirements](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/requirements.html)).

## 4. How D grades and a sub-2.0 GPA cut into transferable credit

**First, separate attempted from earned credit.** Under standard credit-hour rules, F and W courses are attempted but not earned, and an F normally stays in the GPA until a repeat replaces it. The "~84 credits" figure must be split before any university can estimate anything. Only **earned** credits (D or better) are candidates for transfer anywhere. Several systems count only courses at 60%, C or C- and above, and UND carries transferable F grades into the admission GPA ([UND catalog](https://und-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduateacademicinformation/newundergraduatestudentinformation/admissionoftransferstudents/)). NGU's own grading table, probation rules and repeat policy were **not found in any public source**. The student must request an official status statement (بيان حالة) showing earned credits, attempted credits, CGPA and probation status.

**The pivotal unknown: what percentage a D represents at NGU.** Egypt's reported 2025/26 transfer rules require **no course below 60%** and a **CGPA of at least 2.0** ([Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818)). The 2021 Council rules add a **50% cap** on transferred credit and a **3-year minimum** at the receiving college for programmes longer than 4 years ([Ahram Gate](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2950190.aspx)). Older coverage said the GPA floor was left to each university's bylaws. The 2025/26 report states 2.0 as a national condition. If NGU's D band starts at 60% or higher, D courses become eligible in principle for domestic transfer. If it starts lower, as on many US-style scales, they do not. For comparison, Texas A&M–Kingsville defines D as "Passing, 60–69" ([TAMUK grades](https://catalog.tamuk.edu/undergraduate/academic-regulations/grades-academic-standing/)). That one number decides the domestic route, and it also shapes how UK and German assessors read the transcript.

**What GPA repair actually takes (illustrative arithmetic, not NGU policy).** Assume all 84 credits sit in the GPA.

| Current CGPA | Adding 18 new credits (one semester): average needed | Adding 36 new credits (two semesters): average needed | Replacing 3-credit D grades with C grades (if NGU replaces grades) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.80 | ≈2.93 (B) | ≈2.47 | 6 courses |
| 1.85 | ≈2.70 | ≈2.35 | 5 courses |
| 1.90 | ≈2.47 | ≈2.23 | 3 courses |

**Repeating and improving existing D and F courses is the fastest lever**, provided NGU replaces the grade rather than averaging it.

**How each candidate treats D grades and a GPA below 2.0:**

**UH-GAF and TKH-Coventry** use UK learning-outcome APL. A D is a pass on the NGU transcript, but UK panels also look at overall performance. The only published UK numbers for overseas transfers are 55% with no failed modules (Greenwich), 65%/"Good" for Egyptian degree holders (USW) and 75–80% or GPA 3.0 (Kent), all well above this record ([Greenwich](https://www.gre.ac.uk/undergraduate-courses/business-school/business-studies-year-3-direct-entry); [USW Egypt](https://www.southwales.ac.uk/international/your-country/egypt/); [Kent Egypt](https://www.kent.ac.uk/internationalstudent/countries/egypt.html)). Both branch campuses also put a foundation year before Level 4 ([UH-GAF EEE](https://www.gaf.edu.eg/programmes/beng-electrical-and-electronic-engineering); [TKH FAQ](https://tkh.edu.eg/faq/)). NGU's first year (calculus, physics, English) will most likely map to that foundation year. Years 2–3 (circuits, electronics, signals, programming) are the realistic source of Level 4 credit. A low GPA could block admission only if the branch campus applies the national 2.0 rule, which is unknown.

**Hertfordshire UK, LSBU and Teesside** decide "individually". LSBU's Year 2 route needs 120 Level 4 credits "from a directly-equivalent degree course" ([LSBU course spec](https://www.lsbu.ac.uk/_resources/pdf/course-specification/2024-2025/undergraduate/eng/BEng_Hons_Electrical_and_Electronic_Engineering_All-Modes_04_11_2024.pdf)). Teesside's top-up needs a full Level 5 qualification, which a D-average transcript is unlikely to be judged equivalent to. The practical fallback at all three is Year 1 entry, where NGU grades stop mattering for admission but no time is saved.

**Debrecen** sets content rules, not grade rules. It requires ≥75% equivalence, counts only subjects completed in a university programme, and caps transfer at 60% ([UniDeb](https://management.unideb.hu/en/credit-transfer); [UniDeb Faculty](https://eng.unideb.hu/node/286)). D grades are not excluded on paper, but they are not confirmed as accepted either. No GPA floor is stated. The entrance exams are the de facto filter.

**German FHs (HSHL)** apply the strongest law in the student's favour. Credits are recognised unless the university proves a "substantial difference" ([Uni Marburg](https://www2.hrz.uni-marburg.de/de/universitaet/administration/amtliche-mitteilungen/jahrgang2012/58_2012.pdf)). The modified Bavarian formula maps the foreign lowest pass to German 4.0, so a D at the pass mark converts to a pass ([Leuphana](https://www.leuphana.de/einrichtungen/international-center/wege-in-die-welt/angebote-fuer-studierende/studium-als-free-mover/anerkennung-und-notenumrechnung/notenumrechnung.html)). The catch is that failed attempts elsewhere can count against the limited exam attempts ([Uni Marburg 42/2012](https://www.uni-marburg.de/de/universitaet/administration/amtliche-mitteilungen/jahrgang2012/42_2012.pdf)). NGU F grades could therefore become a liability.

**UND and YSU** accept D grades as transfer credit by published policy ([UND](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/requirements.html); [YSU](https://catalog.ysu.edu/undergraduate/general-information/admission/transfer-applicants/)). Engineering majors commonly require C in major courses, so D-graded engineering courses may still have to be retaken. Both enforce a 2.0 admission GPA, so they are closed until the repair is done. A credential evaluator will almost certainly reproduce NGU's D grades and GPA as they stand. WES course-by-course costs $239 ([WES](https://www.wes.org/evaluations/)) and ECE $199 plus add-ons ([ECE](https://www.ece.org/ECE/Individuals/Services--Fees)).

**BUE and MSA** inherit the national rules: GPA ≥2.0, every transferred course ≥60%, and at least 3 years at the receiving college. They are closed today. Even after repair they offer no time saving over finishing at NGU ([BUE bylaws](https://www.bue.edu.eg/uploads/editor/%284-4-2021%29%20FEEE%20BYLAWS%205%20programs.pdf); [Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818)).

**CQU and TU Dublin** credit "pass or higher" or proven learning outcomes. A D may count on CQU's plain wording, but only a written pre-enrolment assessment settles it ([CQU](https://www.cqu.edu.au/study/international/credit-transfers)).

Three patterns stand out:

- **Time is fairly flat.** Nearly every credible option leaves **4–6 semesters**, because foundation years and content matching absorb NGU's first year and D grades limit the rest. The only shorter routes (TU Dublin Year 3, Teesside top-up) lead to technologist awards that the SCU and Syndicate are likely to reject. The Syndicate already refuses Computers & Information graduates and is moving technologists toward a separate syndicate ([Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/4468831); [Riad & Riad](https://riad-riad.com/establishment-of-technologist-syndicate-in-egypt/)).
- **Cost separates the options sharply.** Studying in Egypt (UH-GAF, TKH, NGU) costs a fraction of the UK or Australia once living costs are added. UK maintenance alone is £10,539–£13,761 for nine months ([OTS Solicitors](https://www.otssolicitors.co.uk/news/student-visa-maintenance-funds-increase-from-30-november-2026/)), and Australia requires AUD 29,710 a year ([Navitas](https://www.navitas.com/study/apply/genuine-student/financial-capacity/)).
- **Recognition separates the options most of all.** Degrees earned abroad go to the SCU "case by case, after graduation". The SCU checks full-time regular attendance, home-ministry recognition and, for students studying abroad, a Thanaweya score no more than 5% below the private-university cutoff for their year ([Youm7, April 2026](https://www.youm7.com/amp/2026/4/18/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B6-%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%A8%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-5/7381587)). Since 2025/26 it also requires the maths stream for engineering abroad and a travel permission from the MoHE Missions administration ([Masrawy, April 2026](https://www.masrawy.com/news/education-universityeducation/details/2026/4/20/2975287/%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9)).

No published SCU rule addresses students who carry Egyptian credit into a foreign degree, or sets a minimum residency share. A degree earned mostly by transfer therefore carries unquantified risk. The branch campuses sidestep most of this.

## 7. Contact these three first

**First, UH-GAF admissions.** It is the recommended destination, and its answers on IFP waiver, entry level, D-grade treatment and whether national transfer rules apply decide the plan.

**Second, TKH-Coventry admissions.** It is the near-equal alternative with the strongest recognition record. Ask specifically whether evaluation can happen **before** the student submits an NGU withdrawal letter.

**Third, the University of Hertfordshire's UK transfer/APL team.** It is the same awarding university as UH-GAF. A Hatfield APL opinion on the NGU transcript shows how Hertfordshire reads D grades, and it gives the shortest-time option (Year 2) if the family can fund about £60k.

Send all three emails in the same week, with identical attachments, so the replies are comparable. If budget allows a fourth, the **University of Debrecen** is worth writing to for its pre-transfer offer.

## 10. Documents to obtain from NGU before applying

Request these from the NGU Registrar and the School of Engineering before sending the emails, because every receiving institution asks for some combination of them. NGU's own transfer checklist shows that receiving universities expect an authenticated transcript plus the grading scale ([NGU acknowledgement letter](https://ngu.edu.eg/wp-content/uploads/Fall2023AckLetter.pdf)).

- [ ] **Official transcript** in English, sealed and stamped. It should list every attempt, including F, W and repeated courses, and print **the grading scale with the percentage band for each letter grade** (the 60%-per-course question depends on this).
- [ ] **Status statement (بيان حالة)** showing earned credits, attempted credits, CGPA, academic standing (probation or not) and expected graduation requirements.
- [ ] **Course descriptions / syllabi (توصيف المقررات)**, stamped, for every passed course: learning outcomes, topics, textbooks and assessment breakdown.
- [ ] **Credit-hour breakdown** per course (lecture, tutorial and **lab hours**). Lab content is often what blocks equivalence.
- [ ] **CCAS study plan and programme bylaws (اللائحة الداخلية)**, including the total credits to graduate, and the SCU/ministerial decree for the School of Engineering (Decree 1951 of 2021) and, if available, for the CCAS bylaws ([NGU School of Engineering](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-academic/school-of-engineering/)).
- [ ] **NGU academic regulations**: the grading table, repeat/grade-replacement policy, and probation and dismissal rules.
- [ ] **Enrolment / good-standing letter** and a statement of no disciplinary action. Request a **withdrawal letter only after** a written offer, unless a university insists on it earlier.
- [ ] **Attested and translated copies**: MoHE/MFA attestation of the transcript and certificates where required, and certified translations of any Arabic documents.
- [ ] **Secondary-school certificate and score** (Thanaweya Amma maths stream, or IGCSE/AS/A-Level), plus an **official statement of the score versus the private-university engineering minimum for the certificate year**. For study abroad, the SCU rejects certificates more than 5% below that cutoff ([Youm7, April 2026](https://www.youm7.com/amp/2026/4/18/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B6-%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%A8%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-5/7381587)). Domestic transfer rules also require meeting the target faculty's minimum for that year ([Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818)).
- [ ] **Passport** valid for the full study period, plus passport photos.
- [ ] **English test result** (IELTS 6.0–6.5 depending on the university), if NGU's English-medium letter is not accepted.
- [ ] **For US options only:** a course-by-course evaluation with GPA from the agency the school names, ordered once ([WES](https://www.wes.org/evaluations/); [ECE](https://www.ece.org/ECE/Individuals/Services--Fees)).

**Obtain a written SCU opinion before enrolling anywhere outside Egypt or outside an already-equated branch programme.** Submit the specific university, programme title, duration and the planned NGU credit carry-over through the [SCU e-equivalency portal](https://equ.scu.eg/GuideLines.aspx) or the [SCU equivalency administration](https://scu.eg/en/administrations/general-administration-for-academic-degree-equivalency/). Ask whether registration with the MoHE Missions administration is required before travel, since equivalency is refused to students who did not follow the rules for enrolling abroad ([See-Edu](https://see-edu.news/2021/11/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%8A%D9%8F%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7)). For the branch campuses, ask the institution to state in writing that the equalisation and Syndicate approval cover students admitted by transfer.

## Conclusion

The search for a "lenient" university abroad mostly turned up a trade between leniency and recognition. Every option that would accept this transcript as it stands either sits in a jurisdiction Egypt does not recognise or delivers online study the SCU now warns against. The leverage therefore lies less in finding the right foreign university than in two cheap, local actions:

- **Raise the NGU CGPA to 2.0.** By the arithmetic above, that takes roughly 3–6 repeated D-to-C courses, or one strong semester.
- **Learn NGU's percentage band for a D.** Together with the GPA, it unlocks or closes the Egyptian transfer rules, the US on-campus options and much of the UK APL case at once.

The branch campuses then convert that repaired record into a UK-assessed, Egyptian-recognised degree without the visa and living costs that roughly triple the price of the same award abroad.

A second point is that time saved is likely to be modest everywhere. Foundation years and content matching absorb NGU's first year, so "not restarting from year 1" realistically means **2–3 more years**, not one. The decision should therefore hinge on which written evaluation credits the most courses and offers the more forgiving assessment regime, not on advertised transfer friendliness. A student who collects the Section 10 documents and three written evaluations before paying any non-refundable fee will be choosing on evidence rather than marketing.
