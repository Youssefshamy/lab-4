# Action pack: three emails to send

Send all three in the same week, with the same attachments, so the answers can be compared side by side. Fill in every `[bracket]` first.

**Attach to emails 2 and 3:**
- official NGU transcript, including NGU's grading scale with the percentage band for each letter grade
- course descriptions or syllabi for every course you passed
- credit-hour breakdown and the CCAS study plan

---

## Email 1: NGU (your graduation plan, the number to beat)

**To:** NGU Registrar / Office of Student Affairs, and copy your academic advisor
**Subject:** Request for written graduation plan and academic regulations – [Full Name], ID [NGU Student ID], CCAS

> Dear Registrar and Dr. [Advisor Name],
>
> I am [Full Name], a third-year student in Computer Communications and Autonomous Systems (Student ID [NGU Student ID]). My current cumulative GPA is [x.xx]. I would be grateful for written answers to the following:
>
> 1. How many credit hours and which courses do I still need to graduate, and in how many semesters can I realistically finish?
> 2. When I repeat a course, does the new grade replace the old one in my cumulative GPA? Is there a limit on how many courses I can repeat?
> 3. What is the minimum cumulative GPA required to graduate, and what are the current probation rules?
> 4. What percentage range does each letter grade represent (in particular D and D+)? Please include this grading scale on my official transcript.
> 5. Could I please have an official stamped transcript and official course descriptions for all courses I have taken? What is the fee and processing time?
>
> Thank you very much for your help.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Full Name] — [Phone] — [NGU email]

## After the replies arrive

1. Write down three numbers: semesters left at NGU, at UH-GAF and at TKH, plus total cost for each.
2. If UH-GAF or TKH saves you **at least a year**:
   - get a written opinion from Egypt's Supreme Council of Universities (https://equ.scu.eg/GuideLines.aspx) before enrolling;
   - then accept the offer and only after that withdraw from NGU.
3. Otherwise, stay at NGU and finish. Keep repeating your weakest courses so your GPA stays above 2.0.
