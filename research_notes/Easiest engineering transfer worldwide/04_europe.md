## Q1. Does European recognition law (Lisbon Convention / "substantial differences" / ECTS) favour a student whose courses are passed with D grades?

### Takeaway
Partly yes. In Germany the legal test is learning outcomes and "substantial differences", not grade level, and the standard grade-conversion formula maps the lowest passing grade to a German 4.0 ("ausreichend", a pass). So a passed D course is in principle recognisable. But many universities outside Germany add content-equivalence thresholds (often 75%), caps (e.g., max 60%), or one-time-only windows. Some Dutch schools grant exemptions without shortening the programme. Grade level is rarely the stated barrier. Course-content match, lab components and administrative windows are the real constraints.

### Cited Findings
- Under the Lisbon Convention, a substantial difference exists when recognition "would jeopardise achievement of the purpose sought by such recognition". The concept replaced the older "equivalence" principle. — [Hochschule Kaiserslautern, Recognition of credits](https://hs-kl.de/en/international/international-office/go-abroad/studies-out/recognition-of-credits); [FDFA Belgium, Lisbon Convention](https://www.fdfa.be/en/convention-of-the-recognition-of-qualifications-concerning-higher-education-in-the-european-region)
- The German Rectors' Conference recommends non-recognition only where there are significant differences, e.g. in learning outcomes or focus. Minor differences in scope (e.g. 4 ECTS vs 5 ECTS module) are not significant at one German university. — [search extracts from Uni Passau recognition pages](https://www.geku.uni-passau.de/en/study/examinations/recognition); [TUM archive](https://portal.mytum.de/archiv/komp_ssz/ArchiveFolder_20210325_150056/20210325_163033/1)
- Burden of proof sits with the university. "Kann die Hochschule den wesentlichen Unterschied nicht nachweisen, sind die Studienzeiten, Studienleistungen und Prüfungsleistungen anzurechnen." Equivalence is judged on "Qualifikationsziel, Umfang und Anforderungen". — [Uni Marburg Amtliche Mitteilungen 58/2012](https://www2.hrz.uni-marburg.de/de/universitaet/administration/amtliche-mitteilungen/jahrgang2012/58_2012.pdf)
- German grade conversion uses the "modified Bavarian formula": x = 1 + 3 × (Nmax − Nd)/(Nmax − Nmin). Nmin is the lowest passing grade of the foreign system, so a grade exactly at the pass mark converts to 4.0 (pass). anabin/KMK recommendations can override the formula. — [Leuphana Notenumrechnung](https://www.leuphana.de/einrichtungen/international-center/wege-in-die-welt/angebote-fuer-studierende/studium-als-free-mover/anerkennung-und-notenumrechnung/notenumrechnung.html); [Uni Göttingen](https://uni-goettingen.de/de/132776.html); [FH Westküste UmrechnungsRL 2025](https://www.fh-westkueste.de/fileadmin/Dateien/Hochschule/Rechtliches/Studierende/2025/UmrechnungsRL_2025.pdf); [FAU](https://www.phil.fau.de/files/2016/01/Umrechnung-ausländischer-Noten.pdf)
- In Germany, recognised credits normally also count as study periods. Placement into a higher semester ("Fachsemester") is based on the amount of recognised ECTS, not on semesters attended. — [Uni Kassel, Anerkennung und Anrechnung](https://www.uni-kassel.de/uni/studium/vor-der-bewerbung/rund-um-die-studienplatzbewerbung/wechsel-und-quereinstieg/anerkennung-und-anrechnung.html)
- At Constructor University (Bremen, private), grades are not converted. Accepted modules appear as "TC" (transfer credit). — [Constructor transfer FAQ (search extract)](https://constructor.university/transfer-student-faq)
- Content-equivalence thresholds elsewhere:
  - University of Debrecen requires a "minimum 75% equivalency" between the prior subject and the UD course. This comes from a university-wide rule cited on a management-faculty page. — [UniDeb credit transfer](https://management.unideb.hu/en/credit-transfer)
  - A Spanish university normativa uses at least 75% correspondence of competences and contents. — [UFV normativa grado 24-25](https://recursos.ufv.es/docs/normativa-reconocimiento-de-creditos-grado-24-25.pdf)
- Spain: Real Decreto 822/2021 art. 10 obliges each university to adopt its own recognition rules for Spanish or foreign official degrees. Art. 10.9 recognises up to all basic-training ("formación básica") credits between degrees in the same field. Non-official studies and work experience are capped at 15% combined. — [UAM normativa](https://www.uam.es/uam/estudios/grado/matricula/normativa-academica); [UPSA normativa](https://adminpro.upsa.es/uploads/Normativa_de_Reconocimiento_y_Transferencia_de_creditos_1436ffdf52.pdf)

### Inferences
- If NGU's transcript legend defines D (1.0/4.0) as the lowest passing grade, the Bavarian formula would convert D to German 4.0 and D+ (1.3) to about 3.7. (Preliminary estimate; the Nmin chosen for NGU is "Unknown – requires written confirmation" from the receiving Prüfungsamt or anabin.) The recognised D courses would count as passes but would pull the final German grade toward 4.0. Since prestige is irrelevant, this is acceptable.
- The hypothesis "any passed course may be recognised regardless of grade" is **supported by published policy for Germany** (learning-outcome test plus grade conversion anchored on the pass mark). It is **only partially supported** elsewhere: the 75% content-match rules (Hungary/Spain) and the Dutch "exemptions don't shorten the programme" practice (Q3) limit the benefit.
- Grade requirements may still appear in institution-specific rules. Some private or US-style schools require C or better for transfer credit. Constructor's minimum grade was **not confirmed**.

### Gaps
- I found no official document that explicitly states "D/pass grades are recognised" for any target university. Verify by asking each Prüfungsamt or admissions office in writing: "Is a course passed with grade D (1.0/4.0) eligible for Anrechnung/recognition?"
- No authoritative source was found for converting NGU US-style credit hours to ECTS (the brief's "×2" is unverified). Universities convert by workload or module, not by fixed ratio. Ask each receiving university.

## Q2. Germany (public Hochschulen / FH): eligibility for an Egyptian applicant, concrete programmes, costs, retake rules, dropout risk

### Takeaway
Germany is the best match for the "recognition by learning outcomes, pass = pass" hypothesis, and NRW public universities of applied sciences charge essentially no tuition. Two eligibility hurdles remain. First, the Egyptian school certificate (Thanaweya Amma) generally needs Studienkolleg unless university study in Egypt opens direct access, and NGU's anabin status is unconfirmed. Second, the €11,904/year blocked account. The biggest academic risk is the German exam system: limited attempts, prior failed attempts can be counted, and an estimated ~half of international bachelor students drop out.

### Cited Findings
**Access (HZB) for Egyptian applicants**
- Thanaweya Amma alone generally does not give direct access. Most Egyptians need Studienkolleg, and the threshold cited is ≥65% for Studienkolleg/Feststellungsprüfung eligibility. One to two semesters at a recognised Egyptian university may allow conditional admission, with direct entry or Studienkolleg depending on grades. **Commercial/secondary source; verify on anabin/uni-assist.** — [studienkolleg.org guide for Egyptian students](https://www.studienkolleg.org/blog/studienkolleg-guide-aegyptische-studenten-komplett/)
- Official check tool: uni-assist "Check Hochschulzugang" (https://www.uni-assist.de/tools/check-hochschulzugang/). The Goethe-Institut Egypt Studienkolleg FSP can be repeated at most twice. — [Goethe-Institut FAQ Studienkolleg](https://goethe.de/ins/ae/en/spr/klg/faq.html)
- uni-assist evaluates foreign school and university certificates for equivalence, but the admission decision is made by the university. — [uni-assist profile](https://uni-assist.de/en/about-us/profile)
- NGU's anabin institution status (H+, H+/-) could not be found. anabin status may differ per programme; check anabin.kmk.org or ask ZAB. — [search result summary; anabin category explanation from commercial blog](https://immigrationstartguide.com/blog/anabin-egyptian-universities-germany-blue-card)

**Programmes**
- **Hochschule Hamm-Lippstadt (HSHL), B.Eng. Electronic Engineering:** 7 semesters, 210 ECTS, full-time on campus, started WS 2018/19, accreditation report dated January 2024. — [Akkreditierungsrat document](https://antrag.akkreditierungsrat.de/dokument/91461d13-9b51-4aea-9b54-5c1250b89817). English-taught per aggregator only — [gradgermany](https://www.gradgermany.com/universities/hochschule-hamm-lippstadt?page=2). Aggregators also list no tuition, a semester contribution of about €310, and application via uni-assist — [mygermanuniversity](https://www.mygermanuniversity.com/cities/Hamm). Not confirmed on an official HSHL page.
- **Hochschule Rhein-Waal (Kleve/Kamp-Lintfort), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (B.Sc., formerly "Electronics"):** examination regulations from 2018, with a consolidated version incorporating the 3rd amendment of 19.06.2024. Recognition is governed by the university's Rahmenprüfungsordnung (not retrieved). — [HSRW PO 2018](https://www.hochschule-rhein-waal.de/sites/default/files/documents/2018/04/26/20180426_po_eee_amtl_bek.pdf); [HSRW download](https://www.hochschule-rhein-waal.de/de/media/10057/download)
- TH Deggendorf, Hochschule Bremen and TH-specific higher-semester rules were **not retrieved** (domains failed). THD publishes an English recognition guideline — [leitfaden-anerkennung-qualifikationen_en.pdf](https://cs-mariakirchen.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Hochschulrecht/leitfaden-anerkennung-qualifikationen_en.pdf) (content not verified).

**Costs**
- Blocked account (Sperrkonto) for a student visa (§16b AufenthG) in 2026: €11,904 per year (€992 per month). **Commercial sources**; the German mission may require a different amount. — [bankdaten.de](https://www.bankdaten.de/ratgeber/sperrkonto.html); [study-abroad.org](https://www.study-abroad.org/de/blog/sperrkonto-leitfaden-internationale-studierende/); [workingstudentjobs.de](https://www.workingstudentjobs.de/de/tools/sperrkonto-rechner)
- uni-assist charges processing fees, and some universities cover them. — [uni-assist Bearbeitungskosten](https://www.uni-assist.de/bewerben/kosten-zahlen/bearbeitungskosten/)

**Retakes and failed attempts**
- Failed attempts from other universities are counted if the module would have been credited had it been passed ("Fehlversuche in Studiengängen an anderen Hochschulen werden angerechnet, sofern sie im Fall ihres Bestehens angerechnet worden wären"). Prior failed attempts and study duration still count after switching university. — [Uni Marburg 42/2012](https://www.uni-marburg.de/de/universitaet/administration/amtliche-mitteilungen/jahrgang2012/42_2012.pdf); [Uni Saarland Merkblatt Hochschulwechsel](https://uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/fachrichtung/pharmazie/Studieninteressierte/Merkblatt_Hochschulwechsel.pdf)
- Enrolment must be refused where a required exam in the same or a related programme has been "endgültig nicht bestanden" (Rhineland-Palatinate). The Hesse-style wording limits this to failures at universities "im Geltungsbereich des Grundgesetzes", i.e., in Germany. — [search extracts: HS Mainz FAQ](https://hs-mainz.de/en/studium/studiengaenge/technik/gemeinsame-subseiten-technik/transfer-and-recognition-of-credits-at-mainz-university-of-applied-sciences-school-of-engineering/faqs-transfer-and-recognition-of-credits); [Marburg 58/2012](https://www2.hrz.uni-marburg.de/de/universitaet/administration/amtliche-mitteilungen/jahrgang2012/58_2012.pdf)

**Dropout**
- DZHW estimates that about half of international bachelor students with a foreign HZB drop out, compared with 34% of international master students and 31% of German bachelor students (Heublein/Hutzsch/Schmelzer 2022, as reviewed by HoF Halle). DZHW figures are estimates because transfers cannot be tracked precisely. — [HoF Halle review, Ramirez/Böker 2022](https://www.hof.uni-halle.de/web/dateien/pdf/Ramirez_Boeker_2022_Studienvorbereitung_und_Studienerfolg_int_Studierende_in_DE.pdf)
- 43.1% of degree-seeking international students in Germany (WS 2023/24) studied engineering (DAAD/DZHW 2024). — [Wissenschaftsmanagement article](https://www.wissenschaftsmanagement-online.de/system/files/downloads-wimoarticle/Willkommenskultur%20an%20Hochschulen%20WM0125.pdf)
- Non-EU students show a lower tendency to drop out in the first three semesters, plausibly because they lose their residence permit if they stop studying. — same HoF review
- Fachhochschule dropout in Electrical Engineering is about 30% (all students). This **secondary site cites DZHW without a clear reference; use with caution.** — [profiling-institut.de](https://www.profiling-institut.de/studienabbruch/mint/)
- acatech calls engineering dropout a major policy challenge. — [acatech POSITION Studienabbruch](https://www.acatech.de/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/acatech_POSITION_Studienabbruch_Web.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Credit estimate for a German FH (Preliminary estimate):** of 84 NGU credits, engineering-relevant passed courses (math, physics, circuits, programming, signals, digital logic, communications) are the realistic candidates. General education, humanities and courses with substantial lab mismatches are less likely. A plausible range is roughly 30–75 ECTS. That means placement into semester 2–3 of a 210-ECTS (7-semester) B.Eng, leaving about 4.5–6 semesters including the practical/internship semester and thesis. This is not backed by any university's published figure.
- The failed-attempts rule cuts both ways. If the student failed NGU courses that map to German modules, those failures **may** be counted against the (typically three) attempts. Whether foreign failures count is "Unknown – requires written confirmation" per Prüfungsordnung. The "Geltungsbereich des Grundgesetzes" wording suggests some states ignore foreign failures for the enrolment bar.
- University GPA is generally not an admission criterion for higher-semester entry at a German FH. Admission is driven by HZB status and recognised credits. **Not confirmed** for HSHL/HSRW; ask the university.
- Germany is exam-heavy (written end-of-semester exams with limited attempts). No official source was retrieved on assessment mix per programme; check the Modulhandbuch.

### Gaps
- Official HSHL/HSRW/THD/Hochschule Bremen higher-semester ("höheres Fachsemester") procedures, deadlines and English-taught status are unconfirmed (official domains unreachable).
- Whether NGU study (private Egyptian university) gives direct HZB, and whether the student's Thanaweya Amma score (unknown) meets the threshold. Verify with uni-assist VPD/"Check Hochschulzugang" and anabin.
- Exact attempt limits for the named programmes (commonly three, per general practice) are not confirmed. Check each Prüfungsordnung.

## Q3. Netherlands (HBO universities of applied sciences): exemptions policy

### Takeaway
The Dutch HBO route looks poor for this student. HAN states that higher-year entry is "very strict", that most transfer students start in year 1, and that exemptions are granted only after starting and do not shorten the programme. Saxion's shortened route needs 6 completed semesters. Non-EU tuition is around €8,600 per year.

### Cited Findings
- HAN: "The requirements for starting in a higher year at HAN are very strict. Most students have to start in the 1st year even if they have studied a similar degree elsewhere." Exemptions can only be requested after starting and "will not shorten the length of the program". — [HAN admissions requirements (search extract)](https://hanuniversity.com/en/study-and-living/admissions/admissions-requirements); [HAN EEE admission/finances](https://www.hanuniversity.com/en/programs/bachelor/electrical-and-electronic-engineering/fulltime/admission-finances/)
- HAN's examination board grants exemptions. Engineering contact: Examencommissie.engineering@han.nl. — [HAN blog](https://blog3.han.nl/energizer-international/?p=1423)
- Saxion Electrical and Electronic Engineering: non-EU tuition €8,600 per year; non-EU deadline 1 June 2026 for a 1 September 2026 start. The Short Degree Programme is for those who completed at least 6 semesters or hold a related degree. NVAO decision page exists for the programme. — [Saxion EEE page](https://www.saxion.edu/programmes/bachelor/electrical-and-electronic-engineering); [studyinnl.org](https://www.studyinnl.org/dutch-education/studies/electrical-and-electronic-engineering-1710-saxion-university-of-applied-sciences); [NVAO](https://nvao.net/en/decisions/saxion-hogeschool/b-electrical-and-electronic-engineering)

### Inferences
- With about 3 years at NGU (likely under 6 semesters' equivalent once mismatches are removed), the Saxion short route is probably closed. HAN-style exemptions would lighten the workload but not reduce time or money. **Preliminary estimate:** about 4 years remaining in the Netherlands, which fails priorities 1 and 3.

### Gaps
- Hanze and Fontys exemption rules were not retrieved.
- Dutch HBO binding study advice (BSA) thresholds in year 1 are a risk for a weak student but were not researched here. Check the OER of each programme.

## Q4. Eastern/Central Europe (Hungary, Poland, Latvia): transfer into later years

### Takeaway
Hungary and Poland have published, structured transfer and credit-recognition rules with moderate fees. Debrecen even gives transfer students an offer listing validated courses before they transfer, which comes close to the requested "preliminary evaluation". Both rely on content-match rules (Debrecen: 75%; Poznań: matching learning outcomes), not grade level. Latvia's TSI has a formal "starting at later stages" procedure with a paid recognition commission.

### Cited Findings
**Hungary: University of Debrecen, Electrical Engineering BSc**
- 7 semesters / 210 ECTS, English, B2 English required. Admission needs a high-school certificate plus entrance exams in Mathematics and Physics. Tuition USD 7,000 per year, plus USD 150 application fee and USD 350 entrance-procedure fee. Deadline 15 June. — [UniDeb EE BSc](https://edu.unideb.hu/p/electrical-engineering-bsc); [UniDeb fees](https://www.edu.unideb.hu/p/tuition-fee-application-entrance-fee)
- Faculty of Engineering transfer rules:
  - Credit transfer can be requested once, during the first semester.
  - Before transferring, each transfer student gets an offer showing which courses will be validated.
  - Max 60% of programme credits transferable; at least 40% must be completed at UD.
  - Late arrivals have a 14-working-day deadline.
  
  — [UniDeb Faculty of Engineering transfer information](https://eng.unideb.hu/node/286)
- University-wide rule: ≥75% equivalency, and only subjects completed in a university programme count. — [UniDeb credit transfer](https://management.unideb.hu/en/credit-transfer)

**Hungary: Óbuda University (Kandó Kálmán Faculty of Electrical Engineering)**
- Students coming from universities outside Hungary must go through the official admission procedure, not the credit-transfer process. — [Óbuda KVK transfer requirements (search extract)](https://kvk.uni-obuda.hu/en/home-en/transfer-requirements/)
- Prior courses with a corresponding course in the Óbuda curriculum can be accredited, with credit and a grade. Accreditation is possible only once, at enrolment. Deadlines are 15 August (September start) and 15 January (February start), with no late submissions. Required documents: signed request form, transfer table, transcript, course descriptions. Contact: international@kvk.uni-obuda.hu. — [Óbuda KVK credit transfer](https://kvk.uni-obuda.hu/en/credittransfer/)
- Tuition is about €6,000 per year (EE BSc, 42 months). The CS Engineering BSc is €6,400. **Aggregators only.** — [Studee EE BSc](https://studee.com/find-programs/electrical-engineering-bsc--obuda-university); [Bachelorsportal](https://www.bachelorsportal.com/studies/101994/electrical-engineering.html). Stipendium Hungaricum listed for English BSc programmes per aggregator — [Studee](https://studee.com/find-programs/electrical-engineering-instrumentation-and-automation-bsc--obuda-university)

**Poland**
- **Poznań University of Technology:** transfer is possible after passing at least the first semester, with the application due at least 30 days before the semester starts. The dean decides the semester of admission and the "programme differences" to be covered. Credit is given for passed courses with matching learning outcomes, with equivalent ECTS. — [PUT transfer for international students](https://put.poznan.pl/en/admission-for-international-students/tranfer)
- **Lodz University of Technology:** the applicant must have finished at least the first semester and still be a student at the home university when applying. For winter 2026/27, applicants from outside the Polish system had a deadline of 31 July 2026 (vs 16 September for Polish-system applicants). A complete application does not guarantee admission. — [TUL transfer](https://apply.p.lodz.pl/en/transfer); [TUL node 488](https://apply.p.lodz.pl/en/node/488)
- Documents typically required: transcript with hours/grades/ECTS, syllabi, student-status certificate, and sworn/consular translations (example from UPJP2). — [UPJP2 transfer](https://rekrutacja.upjp2.edu.pl/en/international-students/transfer)

**Latvia**
- **TSI (Transport and Telecommunication Institute, Riga):** official "Rules of procedure for starting studies at later stages" allow transfer from another institution. Prior learning is assessed by a Study Results Recognition Commission. Recognition is a paid service, with the fee set by rector's order, under a Cabinet of Ministers regulation. — [TSI rules (PDF)](https://tsi.lv/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/rules_of_procedure_for_starting_studies_at_later_stages_eng.pdf); [TSI regulation amendments](https://tsi.lv/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/afippa_grozijumi_senats-1912_eng.pdf). Full text not opened (domain unreachable).
- **Riga Technical University:** the course-recognition regulation covers courses from the same or higher cycle. It refers to foreign universities with which RTU has mutual-recognition agreements, and its grade-transfer provisions are written for Latvian universities. — [RTU regulation No. 10](https://www.rtu.lv/writable/public_files/RTU_10._regulation_on_the_recognition_of_the_courses.pdf). Foreign qualifications go through AIC (Latvian ENIC-NARIC), per a secondary source. — [studyineurope.eu](https://www.studyineurope.eu/ko/study-in-latvia/admission-requirements/)

### Inferences
- **Debrecen credit estimate (Preliminary estimate):** the cap is 60% of 210 = 126 ECTS. Realistically, at the 75% content-match rule, perhaps 40–80 ECTS from NGU engineering and math courses. That means entering around year 2, with roughly 2.5–3.5 years remaining. The entrance exam in Mathematics and Physics is a hurdle for a weak student.
- Debrecen's "offer before transfer" is the closest found to a binding pre-evaluation before paying full fees. Whether the USD 150/350 fees are payable first is "Unknown – requires written confirmation".
- Óbuda requires foreign applicants to be admitted as new students and then accredit courses once at enrolment. Credits carry over with a grade, so D grades would likely become low Hungarian grades (unconfirmed).
- RTU is probably unsuitable (agreement-based recognition). TSI is a better Latvian candidate but needs written confirmation of fees and the electronics/telecom programme.
- None of these sources mentions a minimum GPA or minimum grade for transfer. GPA <2.0 is **not** stated as a barrier, but this is "Unknown – requires written confirmation".

### Gaps
- Polish tuition for English-taught engineering (Lodz/Poznań) was not retrieved. A Kraków UT fee page exists ([PK tuition fees](https://iro.pk.edu.pl/?p=15820)) but was not read.
- Retake limits and dropout data for Hungarian, Polish and Latvian programmes were not found.
- Czech, Slovak, Lithuanian, Estonian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovenian, Serbian, Georgian and Armenian options were not researched (budget). Treat them as uncovered.
- EUR-ACE labels for any candidate were not verified. Check the ENAEE database (enaee.eu).

## Q5. Southern Europe (Italy, Spain): recognition into later years

### Takeaway
Italy has no "transfer" from a foreign university. The student must be admitted as a new student via Universitaly pre-enrolment and then request recognition of foreign credits for a shortened career ("abbreviazione di carriera"), decided by the course council. Spain delegates recognition to each university under RD 822/2021, often with a 75% content-match criterion. Both are feasible but slow and bureaucratic, and no engineering-specific evidence favouring D grades was found.

### Cited Findings
- Politecnico di Torino: transfer is only possible between Italian universities. Applicants from foreign universities must be admitted, with possible recognition of foreign credits for abbreviazione di carriera, handled by the Ufficio Promozione, Orientamento e Accesso. — [PoliTo trasferimento](https://www.polito.it/didattica/iscriversi-studiare-laurearsi/gestione-carriera/trasferimento-da-e-presso-altri-atenei)
- Course councils decide on validating credits from Italian or foreign universities. Recognition is the destination university's competence. — [UniCampania regolamento CFU 2023](https://www.jeanmonnet.unicampania.it/images/Regolamenti/Regolamento_riconoscimento_CFU_2023_20.06.23.pdf); [UniCh disciplina trasferimenti](https://www.ingegneriabiomedica.unich.it/sites/st02/files/disciplina_trasferimenti_e_passaggi_a.a._22-23_l9.pdf)
- University of Bologna electronics/telecom degree pages contain sections on non-EU admission and foreign-title recognition (content not retrieved). — [UniBo Ingegneria Elettronica](https://corsi.unibo.it/laurea/IngegneriaElettronica/iscriversi)
- Non-EU applicants abroad must pre-enrol on Universitaly, with visa steps at the consulate. A Declaration of Value or CIMEA statement is typically required. — [UniKore](https://unikore.it/en/study/); [studyineurope.eu (secondary)](https://www.studyineurope.eu/study-in-italy/admission-requirements/)
- Spain: RD 822/2021 art. 10 (see Q1). — [UAM](https://www.uam.es/uam/estudios/grado/matricula/normativa-academica)

### Inferences
- Italian state universities are cheap in principle (fees scale with income/ISEE), but **no fee figure was verified here**. Most English-taught engineering in Italy is at master's level per a secondary source, so a bachelor's would likely be in Italian, which is a major barrier for this student. **Preliminary estimate:** poor fit unless the student learns Italian.
- Spain: same language barrier for public universities. Private Spanish universities were not researched.

### Gaps
- Italian tuition by ISEE, English-taught engineering bachelors accepting abbreviazione di carriera, and maximum recognised CFU were not retrieved.
- Portugal, Greece, Malta (MCAST) and the Republic of Cyprus were not researched (budget).
- Spain's regime for partial foreign studies (Real Decreto 889/2022 "convalidación parcial") was not verified.

## Q6. Private / blended European options (IU International University, Constructor University)

### Takeaway
IU (private, state-recognised, Germany) advertises recognition of prior academic work cutting study time by up to 50%, with a free online pre-check. This is the closest match in Europe to "preliminary evaluation before fee". Constructor University gives transfer-credit decisions 4–6 weeks after application, without converting grades, but costs €10,000 per semester. Online IU degrees carry an Egyptian SCU recognition risk.

### Cited Findings
- IU recognition can reduce study time "by up to 50%" depending on prior field. Academic credits from another university are "recognised"; training and work experience are "credited".
