# US and Canada engineering transfer options for an NGU CCAS student (D grades, GPA ~1.8–1.9, ~84 credits)

**Method note (read first):** Direct page fetches (WebFetch and curl) to tesu.edu, excelsior.edu, smartcatalogiq.com and similar university domains were blocked by this environment's egress proxy. That included "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND" errors and "CONNECT 403" responses. Every finding below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, usually the official university or catalog pages. Before anyone relies on a figure, they should open the URL and confirm the exact current wording. Where sources conflicted, both are listed. Today's date is 2026-10-10.

**Labels used:** "Supported by published policy" means an official page states it. "Preliminary estimate" means my arithmetic or inference from published policy. "Unknown – requires written confirmation" means no source was found.

## Q1. Adult and transfer-friendly online institutions (TESU, Excelsior): D-grade policy, ABET-ETAC status, limits, cost

### Takeaway
Thomas Edison State University (TESU) is the only verified US option whose published policy still lets D grades count. It is narrow, though:
- D and D+ grades can count toward general education and free electives, but only if the GPA of all credits applied to the degree is at least 2.0.
- Area-of-study (major) courses and English composition need a C or better.
- The BS in Electronics Systems Engineering Technology is ABET-ETAC accredited and fully online.
- TESU does not issue I-20s, so the student cannot get an F-1 visa through it, which creates a serious Egyptian-recognition risk.

Excelsior University stopped accepting D grades in Spring 2023. It now requires a C- or higher, so Excelsior loses most of its advantage for this student.

### Cited Findings
**Thomas Edison State University (TESU), New Jersey**
- **Degree:** BS in Electronics Systems Engineering Technology. It is 120 credits, delivered online, and accredited by ABET's Engineering Technology Accreditation Commission (ETAC) under the General Criteria and the Electrical/Electronic(s) Engineering Technology Program Criteria. — [TESU program page](https://tesu.edu/ast/programs/bs/electronics-systems-engineering-technology); [TESU catalog – BS ESET](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-applied-sciences/bs-in-electronics-systems-engineering-technology)
- **Area of study:** The current catalog lists a 65-credit area of study covering DC/AC circuits, solid-state devices, digital electronics, microprocessors and electronic instrumentation. It includes 9 elective credits and capstone ELT-4950 (4 credits). 18 area-of-study credits must be at 3000/4000 level. — [TESU catalog](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-applied-sciences/bs-in-electronics-systems-engineering-technology)
- **Required math and science:** Statistics (3), Calculus I (3), Calculus II (3), Physics I with lab (4), Physics II with lab (4), Chemistry I with lab (4), plus computer concepts and a programming or PLC course. These count as general education or electives. — [TESU catalog](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-applied-sciences/bs-in-electronics-systems-engineering-technology)
- **Math advice:** TESU advises students not to take electronics systems area-of-study courses unless their calculus I-level math skills are reasonably current. — [TESU catalog](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-applied-sciences/bs-in-electronics-systems-engineering-technology)
- **Earlier catalog versions differ.** The 2020–21 catalog listed 124 credits with a 49-credit area of study, so requirements change and should be checked. — [TESU 20-21 catalog](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/en/2020-2021/20-21-undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-applied-sciences/bs-in-electronics-systems-engineering-technology)
- **D-grade rule (undergraduate):**
  - D- grades are not accepted.
  - D+ and D grades "may be accepted to non-area of study requirements for most programs, provided the overall GPA of credits applied to the degree is at least 2.0."
  - Area-of-study and required composition courses need a C or better.
  - Sources: the [CollegeTransfer.net TESU profile](https://www.collegetransfer.net/ThomasEdisonStateCollege/TransferProfile/tabid/145/Default.aspx), consistent with the [TESU catalog – Transfer Credit](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/methods-of-learning-and-earning-credit/transfer-credit/) and the [TESU transcripts page](https://tesu.edu/academics/catalog/thomas-edison-state-college-transcripts), which lists D- among grades not accepted.
  - The full official grade table could not be retrieved, so the exact catalog wording should be verified.
- **International credits:**
  - TESU does not evaluate foreign transcripts itself. It accepts course-by-course recommendations from specific agencies only, named in results as ACEI, CAREE and ECE.
  - It will not mix or match evaluations from different agencies, the student pays all evaluation costs, and TESU keeps final say on how much credit is awarded.
  - Credit from international institutions is capped at 90.
  - WES did not appear on the accepted list in search results. Unknown – requires written confirmation.
  - Sources: [TESU – International Credit Evaluations (catalog)](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/graduate-catalog/university-policies-and-procedures/international-student-policies/international-credit-evaluations); [TESU – Apply as an International Student](https://www.tesu.edu/admissions/requirements/international.php)
- **No I-20:** TESU does not issue Certificates of Eligibility (Form I-20), so the student cannot get F-1 status through TESU. Non-native English speakers must submit TOEFL or IELTS scores. — [TESU – Apply as an International Student](https://www.tesu.edu/admissions/requirements/international.php)
- **Residency:**
  - Per-credit students must complete 15 credits through TESU online, Guided Study or e-Pack courses.
  - The requirement can be waived by paying the "Edison Accelerate" fee.
  - TESU states there are no physical residency requirements.
  - Sources: [TESU catalog – Residency Requirements](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/undergraduate-catalog/university-policies-and-procedures/undergraduate-academic-policies/enrollment/residency-requirements); [TESU Credit-Hour Residency FAQs](https://www.tesu.edu/admissions/faqs/credit-hour-residency.php)
- **Tuition:**
  - The official page lists $449 per credit for New Jersey and Pennsylvania residents and $584 per credit for out-of-state students. The fiscal year is not stated on the extract.
  - Third-party estimates are lower ($435/$567 and $427/$556), which conflicts with the official figures.
  - Sources: [TESU undergraduate tuition](https://www.tesu.edu/tuition-financial-aid/tuition-fees/undergraduate.php); [learn.org](https://learn.org/articles/thomas_edison_state_acceptance_rate.html)
  - Whether international online students pay the out-of-state rate is not confirmed.
- **Application fee:** One source says $75 non-refundable and another says $50. This conflict is unresolved. — [TESU – International](https://www.tesu.edu/admissions/requirements/international.php)
- **Transfer pathway context:** A County College of Morris AAS in Electronics Engineering Technology articulates into this BS, and TESU accepts up to 90 credits through that pathway. — [TESU – County College of Morris pathway](https://www.tesu.edu/admissions/transfer-pathways/county-college-of-morris.php)

**Excelsior University, New York**
- **Degree:** BS in Electrical Engineering Technology, accredited by ABET-ETAC. — [Excelsior BSEET program page](https://www.excelsior.edu/program/bachelor-of-science-in-electrical-engineering-technology/); [Excelsior BSEET brochure (PDF)](https://www.excelsior.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Bachelor_of_Science_in_Electrical_Engineering_Technology__Brochure-.pdf)
- **Delivery:** There is a 100% online option and a hybrid option. The hybrid option requires in-person sessions in St. Petersburg, Florida: a three-hour session every other week, about 12 hours on site per course per term. — [Excelsior BSEET explore page](https://explore.excelsior.edu/BEL.html); [Excelsior BSEET program page](https://www.excelsior.edu/program/bachelor-of-science-in-electrical-engineering-technology/)
- **D grades no longer accepted.** Effective Spring 2023, approved transfer credit receives a P grade only for courses graded "C-" or higher. Before Spring 2023, D grades were accepted in transfer. — [Excelsior OneTranscript policy](https://www.excelsior.edu/policy/onetranscript-policy/); [Excelsior Transfer Credit Guidelines](https://www.excelsior.edu/transfer-credit-guidelines/)
- **Grades and GPA:** Transfer credit appears as "P" and does not affect the Excelsior GPA. — [Excelsior Grade Scale/GPA policy](https://www.excelsior.edu/policy/grade-scale-gpa-and-credit-application/)
- **International credentials:** These must go to an evaluation agency recommended by Excelsior, at the student's cost. — [Excelsior Transfer and Evaluated Credit Sources](https://www.excelsior.edu/policy/transfer-credit-sources/)
- **Cost:**
  - Partner page price is $510 per credit (non-partner), with partners saving $100 per credit.
  - The Post-Admission Credit Evaluation Service costs $300.
  - Evaluation submitted with the admission application is free.
  - Source: [Excelsior International Scholarship and Tuition Services partner page](https://www.excelsior.edu/partner/international-scholarship-and-tuition-services/)

**Charter Oak State College (CT):** I found no engineering or ETAC bachelor's in my searches, so I did not research it further. Unknown – requires written confirmation.

### Inferences
- **TESU credit estimate (Preliminary estimate):**
  - NGU courses graded C or better can map to the 65-credit area of study, subject to course matching and 3000/4000-level rules.
  - D/D+ courses can only fill general education and free electives, which is roughly 55 credits in a 120-credit degree.
  - The "applied-credit GPA ≥ 2.0" condition means a mostly-D transcript cannot all be applied. Each D (1.0) must be offset by B grades (3.0) or better, or the student must select which D courses to apply.
  - Realistically, perhaps 30–60 of the 84 credits might apply, with 60–90 credits remaining.
  - At $584 per credit that is about $35k–$53k of tuition, plus TESU fees and evaluation costs. This is not confirmed: the international rate and fees are unknown.
  - The international cap of 90 credits is not the binding constraint. The grade rules are.
- **Egypt recognition:** TESU is online only and cannot sponsor F-1 status, so the degree would be earned while living in Egypt. Per the project brief, Egypt's Supreme Council of Universities (SCU) generally does not recognize online degrees and requires physical residence. That makes Engineers Syndicate registration unlikely for both TESU and Excelsior. In addition, ETAC is "engineering technology", not ABET-EAC "engineering", which further weakens professional recognition.
- **Excelsior:** Under current rules, only NGU courses graded C- or better transfer. For a mostly-D transcript, Excelsior is likely worse than TESU, despite its reputation as the most generous transfer school.

### Gaps
- The exact current TESU catalog text on D grades could not be fetched (domain blocked). Verify at the [TESU Transfer Credit catalog page](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/current/undergraduate-catalog/methods-of-learning-and-earning-credit/transfer-credit/) or by email to TESU Admissions.
- Whether TESU accepts WES evaluations for undergraduate international credit is unknown. The results list only ACEI, CAREE and ECE.
- TESU's international tuition rate and the Annual Tuition Plan price are not confirmed.
- TESU's undergraduate admission GPA requirement is not found. TESU is commonly described as an adult-learner school, but no published GPA minimum was found. Unknown – requires written confirmation.
- Excelsior's international admission rules and BSEET residency and capstone rules were not retrieved.

## Q2. Online ABET-EAC "engineering" bachelor's degrees (ASU, UND) and other online ETAC options (ODU): minimum transfer grade and admission GPA

### Takeaway
- **ASU Online BSE Electrical Engineering (EAC)** is out of reach. It requires a 3.00 transfer GPA, or 2.50 plus 2.75 in critical math and physics courses, and accepts only C- or better courses.
- **UND's online BS Electrical Engineering (ABET, fully online with lab kits)** is the most D-friendly EAC option found. UND defines transferable credit as grade "D" or above, but requires a 2.0 cumulative transferable GPA for applicants with 24+ credits, which the student currently misses. UND also requires 30 credits taken at UND.
- **ODU's online Electrical Engineering Technology (ETAC)** accepts only C or better.

### Cited Findings
**Arizona State University (ASU) Online, BSE Electrical Engineering (ABET-EAC)**
- **Admission:** With 24+ transferable hours, applicants need a minimum 3.00 transfer GPA. The secondary option is a 2.50 transfer GPA plus a 2.75 GPA in MAT 265, MAT 266, PHY 121 and PHY 122. With under 24 hours, the 3.00 GPA plus freshman criteria apply. — [ASU Degree Search – Electrical Engineering BSE](https://degrees.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/ESEEEBSE/electrical-engineering); [ASU Engineering admission requirements](https://engineering.asu.edu/admission-requirements/); [ASU Online EE Power concentration](https://asuonline.asu.edu/online-degree-programs/undergraduate/electrical-engineering-electric-power-and-energy-systems-bse/)
- **Transfer grade:** ASU accepts college-level courses graded "C-" or better. — [ASU transfer admission](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/transfer/admission)
- **English for international applicants:** IELTS 6.5, PTE 58, Duolingo 105 or Cambridge 176. — [ASU transfer admission](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/transfer/admission)

**University of North Dakota (UND), BS Electrical Engineering (online or on campus)**
- **Delivery:** The program is ABET accredited and available 100% online. Online students take the same classes as on-campus students and complete labs with lab kits. The accelerated BS/MS page says students are never required to visit campus. — [UND BSEE](https://und.edu/programs/electrical-engineering-bsee/); [UND "How online works"](https://und.edu/programs/electrical-engineering-bsee/how-online-works.html); [UND EE BS/MS/MEng](https://und.edu/programs/electrical-engineering-bs-ms-meng/)
- **Older source conflicts.** A circa-2015 ASEE paper described UND as "mostly" online with labs on campus. The current UND pages supersede it. — [ASEE paper](https://peer.asee.org/online-delivery-of-an-undergraduate-engineering-program.pdf)
- **Commission not shown on the extract.** The UND page extract said "ABET-accredited". Whether this is EAC is not shown on the extract and should be verified on [ABET search](https://amspub.abet.org/aps/name-search). My expectation is EAC, but it is not confirmed here.
- **Transfer grade:** UND defines transferable credit as "college-level credits completed … with a grade of 'D' or above." UND academic departments decide whether the credits apply to the major. — [UND – How to Transfer College Credits](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/requirements.html)
- **Admission GPA:** Applicants with 24+ transferable credits need a minimum 2.0 cumulative transferable college GPA (2.60 for aviation). — [UND transfer apply page](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/apply.html); [UND catalog – Admission of Transfer Students](https://und-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduateacademicinformation/newundergraduatestudentinformation/admissionoftransferstudents/)
- **F grades:** F grades that would otherwise transfer are carried in the GPA calculation, and a 2.00 (C) average on all university work is needed to earn a degree. — [UND catalog – Admission of Transfer Students](https://und-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduateacademicinformation/newundergraduatestudentinformation/admissionoftransferstudents/)
- **Residency:** A minimum of 30 credits must be taken at UND, including 15 credits in the major. — [UND – transfer requirements](https://und.edu/admissions/transfer/requirements.html)
- **Tuition:** UND's October 2025 undergraduate cost sheet lists an "International Students Online" base tuition of $893 per credit for 2025-26, subject to change for 2026-27. This is provisional, because the extract did not clearly map engineering to that column. UND engineering pages say online students pay the same rate regardless of residency. — [UND counselor cost sheet (PDF)](https://und.edu/admissions/_files/docs/counselorpacket-digitalflyers-8.5x11_v1.pdf); [UND EE BS/MS cost page](https://und.edu/programs/electrical-engineering-bs-ms-meng/cost.html)

**Old Dominion University (ODU) Online, BS Electrical Engineering Technology (computer engineering technology concentration available)**
- **Accreditation:** ABET-ETAC. — [ODU Online EET](https://online.odu.edu/academics/programs/electrical-engineering-technology); [ODU EET program page](https://www.odu.edu/academics/programs/undergraduate/electrical-engineering-technology)
- **Credits and residency:** 128–134 total credits, with at least 30 credits taken at ODU. — [ODU Online EET](https://online.odu.edu/academics/programs/electrical-engineering-technology)
- **Transfer grade:** ODU accepts all transferable credits graded "C" or better. — [ODU Guaranteed Admission / Transfer](https://online.odu.edu/admissions/transfer-admissions/guaranteed-admission); [TransferVirginia ODU profile](https://www.transfervirginia.org/institution-profile/6A67B9A03FC911EBB15B0242AC15010A)
- **Tuition:** Per credit, $281 for Virginia residents and $972 for non-residents. A $453 rate applies to "technology-delivered courses outside Virginia and/or the United States". Which rate an online student in Egypt pays is not confirmed. — [ODU EET program page](https://www.odu.edu/academics/programs/undergraduate/electrical-engineering-technology)
- **On-campus BSCE contrast:** ODU's on-campus Computer Engineering BS requires a 2.00 cumulative GPA and C or better in 200-level ECE and CS courses. — [ODU catalog BSCE](https://catalog.odu.edu/undergraduate/engineering-technology/electrical-computer-engineering/computer-engineering-bsce/)

### Inferences
- **UND is the best online ABET "engineering" fit on paper (Preliminary estimate):**
  - D grades transfer as credit, but EE departmental applicability is uncertain.
  - If admitted, perhaps 40–70 of 84 credits transfer. After the 30-credit residency, and given that EE core courses may need re-taking, roughly 50–80 credits likely remain, about $45k–$71k at $893 per credit.
  - The 2.0 GPA floor is the blocker. If the evaluator-computed GPA, including F grades, stays below 2.0, admission is unlikely unless UND grants an exception.
- **All online options are a problem for Egypt.** Online delivery from Egypt carries the same SCU and Syndicate risk as TESU. No F-1 is involved.
- **ASU is effectively excluded** on both GPA and grade rules.
- **ODU is excluded for D-grade courses** but would accept C/C- courses as credit.

### Gaps
- Whether UND's GPA calculation for an international transcript uses the WES/ECE-computed GPA, and whether exceptions or appeals exist below 2.0. Unknown – requires written confirmation from UND Admissions.
- UND's ABET commission (EAC) and EE-specific minimum grades for prerequisite chains (for example, a C in calculus) were not confirmed.
- ODU's online transfer admission GPA for non-Virginia and international students was not found.
- University of Arizona online and Florida International online engineering were not researched within the tool budget.

## Q3. On-campus US universities: D-grade transfer and admission GPA, and whether below 2.0 is possible

### Takeaway
Every on-campus university checked publishes a transfer admission floor of at least 2.0, and none published an admit-below-2.0 route for engineering:
- Youngstown State, Texas A&M–Kingsville (university level), Wichita State, UND and Prairie View: 2.0.
- University of Bridgeport: 2.5 per its own blog.
- Texas A&M–Kingsville's College of Engineering: 2.5.

Youngstown State and UND accept D grades as transfer credit, but major and engineering courses usually need a C. Prairie View and Bridgeport require a C.

### Cited Findings
**Youngstown State University (YSU), Ohio**
- **D grades:** YSU accepts a "D" grade "on the same basis as the rules governing native students." A D "might not satisfy a prerequisite for which a higher grade is needed", and most major courses have a minimum of C to apply to the degree. — [YSU catalog – Transfer Applicants](https://catalog.ysu.edu/undergraduate/general-information/admission/transfer-applicants/); [YSU Transfer Students](https://ysu.edu/admissions/apply-to-ysu/transfer-students)
- **International admission GPA:** A minimum 2.0 on a 4.0 scale and good disciplinary standing at the previous institution. Some programs require more. — [YSU catalog – International Undergraduate Admission](https://catalog.ysu.edu/undergraduate/general-information/admission/international-undergraduate-admission/)
- **Conditional admission:** YSU's international office has a "Conditional Admission Requirements" page (context: English). — [YSU Conditional Admission](https://ysu.edu/international-programs-office/apply-now/quick-menu/conditional-admission-requirements)
- **Overseas credit and evaluation:** Overseas credit may need an official syllabus or course description, and an international transcript evaluation is required. Engineering students begin as "Pre-Engineering". — [YSU catalog – International Undergraduate Admission](https://catalog.ysu.edu/undergraduate/general-information/admission/international-undergraduate-admission/)
- **ABET status of YSU EE/CompE:** Not confirmed in this research. Verify on [ABET search](https://amspub.abet.org/aps/name-search).

**University of Bridgeport (UB), Connecticut (private, many international students)**
- **Admission GPA:** UB's own blog states a minimum GPA of 2.5 for general transfer admission. US News and CampusReel list 2.0, which conflicts. — [UB – Does My GPA Transfer?](https://www.bridgeport.edu/news/does-my-gpa-transfer/); [US News UB](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges/university-of-bridgeport-1416/applying); [UB transfer admission requirements](https://www.bridgeport.edu/admissions/transfer/admission-requirements)
- **Transfer grade:** College-level courses "will generally transfer, provided that a grade of at least a C (GPA 2.0) is earned". Courses must be 100-level or above. — [UB – How do transfer credit equivalencies work?](https://www.bridgeport.edu/news/how-do-transfer-credit-equivalencies-work/); [UB Transfer Credits](https://www.bridgeport.edu/admissions/transfer/transfer-credit)
- **Credit limits and residency:**
  - At least 12 credits are needed to apply.
  - The maximum is 66 credits from two-year colleges and 90 from four-year institutions.
  - The last 30 semester hours must be taken at UB.
  - Credit must come from a CHEA- or US Department of Education-recognized accredited institution. That last criterion may need interpreting for foreign schools.
  - Source: [UB Transfer Credits](https://www.bridgeport.edu/admissions/transfer/transfer-credit)
- **ABET status of UB EE/CompE:** Not confirmed in this research.

**Texas A&M University–Kingsville (TAMUK)**
- **University-level transfer:** 2.0 cumulative GPA and good standing. — [TAMUK catalog – Transfer Students](https://catalog.tamuk.edu/undergraduate/admission/transfer-students/)
- **College of Engineering:** 2.5 overall GPA from all college work. Applicants with under 60 transferable credits and a 2.0–2.49 GPA may be admitted to General Engineering. — [TAMUK catalog – Transfer Students](https://catalog.tamuk.edu/undergraduate/admission/transfer-students/)
- **D grades:** D = "Passing, 60-69" in TAMUK's grade table. No explicit statement was found on whether D grades transfer. — [TAMUK Grades and Academic Standing](https://catalog.tamuk.edu/undergraduate/academic-regulations/grades-academic-standing/)

**Wichita State University (WSU), Kansas**
- **Admission GPA:** Transfer applicants with 24+ hours need a 2.00 cumulative GPA on all previous college work. The College of Engineering lists 2.00. — [WSU Transfer Admission](https://www.wichita.edu/TransferAdmission)
- **D grades:** The catalog says some programs do not accept transfer courses graded D. An older third-party profile says C is the lowest transferable grade. — [WSU catalog – Transfer Credit](https://catalog.wichita.edu/undergraduate/admission/transfer-credit/); [CollegeTransfer WSU](https://www.collegetransfer.net/WichitaStateUniversity/TransferProfile/tabid/145/Default.aspx)
- **International credit:** The institution must be recognized by its home Ministry of Education. Credit evaluation starts only after admission. — [WSU catalog – Transfer Credit](https://catalog.wichita.edu/undergraduate/admission/transfer-credit/)

**Prairie View A&M University (PVAMU), Texas**
- **Admission GPA:** Transfers with 15+ hours need a 2.00 GPA. — [PVAMU Admissions PDF](https://www.pvamu.edu/academicaffairs/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/Admissions%20Information%20and%20Requirements%20Undergraduate.pdf)
- **Engineering:** Transfers with 30+ hours need a C or higher in all transfer courses and a 2.5 GPA in math, science and engineering courses. — [PVAMU College of Engineering – Future Students](https://www.pvamu.edu/cheg/future_students/)
- **D grades:** Only C or above transfers. The one exception is a D in a sequential course followed by a B or better in the next course at the same institution. — [PVAMU catalog – Undergraduate Information](https://catalog.pvamu.edu/admissionsinformationandrequirements/undergraduate_information/)

**General engineering probation context:** Several engineering colleges use 2.0 as the probation trigger, and UT Arlington notes some departments require 2.5. These are rules for enrolled students, not admission routes. — [UMD Engineering probation](https://eng.umd.edu/services/academic-probation); [UTA transition to engineering](https://www.uta.edu/academics/schools-colleges/engineering/students/advising/transition-to-engineering)

**Community-college route:**
- Some community colleges set no minimum high-school GPA for international applicants, while others require 2.0. This source is from 2019. — [US News 2019](https://www.usnews.com/education/community-colleges/articles/2019-07-09/what-international-students-should-know-about-community-college-admissions)
- Community colleges want NACES/AICE course-by-course evaluations with GPA calculation, for example Johnson County CC. — [JCCC transfer credits](https://www.jccc.edu/admissions/international/transfer-credits.html)
- GPA at a new school generally resets, but receiving universities may still weigh the prior record. — [Appily](https://www.appily.com/guidance/articles/applying-to-college/transferring-college-credits)

### Inferences
- **No on-campus ABET engineering program was found that publishes admission below 2.0 for transfers.** The realistic routes are:
  - (a) A credential evaluator recomputes the GPA at or above 2.0. This is possible if F or withdrawn courses are treated differently, but it is uncertain.
  - (b) Enroll at an open-admission community college on F-1 (no GPA minimum at some), earn a strong new GPA, then transfer. However, UND, WSU and TAMUK compute GPA on "all previous college work", so foreign grades may still be averaged in.
  - (c) An individual appeal. TAMUK says applicants not meeting criteria "do not qualify for automatic admission", which implies review is possible.
- **Best on-campus D-tolerant candidates:** YSU (D accepted on native basis, 2.0 international floor, pre-engineering start) and UND on-campus (D or above transferable).
- **Credits (Preliminary estimate):** For either school, perhaps 30–60 of 84 credits count, because engineering courses with D grades will likely need to be retaken to meet C prerequisites. That leaves about 4–6 semesters on campus.
- **Excluded or weakest for this profile:** PVAMU and UB (C required). TAMUK engineering requires 2.5, though TAMUK General Engineering is possible at 2.0–2.49 only with under 60 credits.
- **On-campus is the only US route compatible with Egypt's residence requirement,** because it means physical residence on F-1.

### Gaps
- ABET-EAC status and tuition for YSU, UB, TAMUK, WSU and PVAMU EE/CompE programs were not verified in this pass. Use [ABET program search](https://amspub.abet.org/aps/name-search).
- Not researched within the tool budget: University of the District of Columbia, Tennessee State, Alabama A&M, Southern University, Gannon and Lawrence Tech.
- No published "conditional admission below 2.0" for transfer engineering students was found at any school.
- Per-school international tuition and living costs are not collected. Unknown – requires checking each bursar page.

## Q4. Foreign credential evaluation (WES/ECE): fees and how Egyptian D grades map

### Takeaway
- WES Course-by-Course (ICAP) costs $239 USD in 2026.
- ECE Course-by-Course has a $199 base price, about $281 with typical add-ons.
- Schools differ on which evaluator they accept. TESU names ECE but not WES, and won't mix evaluators.
- An evaluated D is still a D. Credit is awarded or refused by the receiving school, not by WES or ECE.

### Cited Findings
- **WES Course-by-Course ICAP:** $239 USD (C$284). Basic is $186 USD. Delivery and other fees are extra, and a 3% increase took effect 1 January 2026. — [WES Credential Evaluations and Fees](https://www.wes.org/evaluations/)
- **WES conflicting figure:** A third-party article says $214. — [GradRight](https://gradright.com/what-is-wes-evaluation-how-much-are-wes-fees/)

## Q6. Canada: advanced standing, minimum grades, CEAB, study-permit cap

### Takeaway
Canada looks poorly suited to a below-2.0, D-heavy transcript:
- The best-known degree-completion pathway, Lakehead's College Transfer B.Eng., requires a 65–70% average (GPA 2.5–3.0) and industry experience. International diploma holders must contact the faculty individually.
- BCIT's Mechanical B.Eng. does not take external applicants, and its Civil B.Eng. gives transfer credit only for equivalent outcomes.
- Bachelor's applicants need a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) under the 2026 cap.

### Cited Findings
- **Lakehead College Transfer B.Eng. entry requirements:**
  - Lakehead's own page lists a 65% average (GPA 2.5) plus one or more years of relevant industry experience after an Engineering Technology diploma.
  - The 2023 transfer guide and ONTransfer list 70% (GPA 3.0) plus two or more years of experience, which conflicts.
  - International diploma holders are told to contact the Faculty of Engineering.
  - Applicants not meeting requirements "will be evaluated on an individual basis".
  - Sources: [Lakehead College Transfer Program](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/faculties/engineering/college-transfer-program); [Lakehead admissions to B.Eng.](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/faculties/engineering/college-transfer-program/admissions); [ONTransfer listing](https://ontransfer.ca/i/ontransfer+pathways_search_details?guid=P-10175-S-2-CA00016-R-1-LAKE)
- **Lakehead programme structure:** Entry is into third year after a compulsory July–August transition programme. Missing core courses must be made up. — [Lakehead College Transfer Program](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/faculties/engineering/college-transfer-program)
- **BCIT:**
  - The Mechanical Engineering B.Eng. does not accept external applicants, who are directed to the diploma instead. — [BCIT Mech B.Eng.](https://www.bcit.ca/programs/mechanical-engineering-bachelor-of-engineering-full-time-8020beng/)
  - The Civil B.Eng. is open to international applicants and offers transfer credit for equivalent coursework. — [BCIT Civil B.Eng.](https://www.bcit.ca/programs/civil-engineering-bachelor-of-engineering-full-time-8660beng/)
  - Foreign education may need an ICES assessment. — [BCIT Transfer Credit](https://www.bcit.ca/admission/entrance-requirements/transfer-credit/)
- **2026 study-permit cap (secondary sources):**
  - 408,000 study permits in total (155,000 new and 253,000 extensions), of which 180,000 require a PAL or TAL.
  - 309,670 application spaces.
  - Provincial splits for permits and spaces: Ontario 70,074 and 104,780; BC 24,786 and 32,596; Alberta 21,582 and 32,271.
  - Master's and PhD students at public institutions are PAL-exempt from 1 January 2026. Bachelor's students are not.
  - A PAL is valid until 31 December of its issue year.
  - Sources: [immigration.ca](https://immigration.ca/canada-sets-2026-international-student-cap-with-fewer-permits-and-stricter-provincial-limits/); [canadasmarts PAL guide](https://canadasmarts.com/provincial-attestation-letter-canada-study-permit/)
  - Cost-of-living proof figures conflict between sources ($22,895 vs $23,448).

### Inferences
- **CEAB B.Eng. transfer for this student is unlikely.** Canadian B.Eng. advanced standing typically needs grades well above D, and the Lakehead bar is 65–70%. The Lakehead pathway also expects a Canadian-style technology diploma plus work experience.
- **A Canadian college diploma (Electronics Engineering Technology) is a more plausible lower-bar route,** but it would add 2–3 years, requires a PAL, and the student would not earn an engineering degree. These are preliminary inferences, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No published Canadian minimum transfer grade was found for Lakehead, BCIT or Ontario/BC polytechnic B.Eng. or B.Tech. programmes. Likely 60% or C, but unconfirmed.
- CEAB accreditation lists were not retrieved. Check the [Engineers Canada accredited programs list](https://engineerscanada.ca/accreditation/accredited-programs).
- Lakehead and BCIT international tuition were not collected.
