## A1. NGU CCAS program: structure, school, approval, partnership

### Takeaway
CCAS ("Computers, Communications and Autonomous Systems") is one of the programmes in NGU's School of Engineering. The school was set up by Ministerial Decree 1951 (June 2021), and the programme was co-designed with UCL Engineering. NGU awards the degree, not UCL. UCL describes the programme as five years long, while an NGU 2023/24 fee announcement lists Engineering as 8 semesters, so the length is unclear. The total credit hours and the year-by-year study plan are not published in any source found.

### Cited Findings
- NGU's School of Engineering offers three programmes, one of them CCAS. First year covers fundamentals (mathematics, physics, programming) and then moves into electronics. Students later choose a communications path or an autonomous-systems path. — [NGU School of Engineering](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-academic/school-of-engineering/)
- NGU describes CCAS as covering electronics, computer networking, artificial intelligence and control systems, aimed at cloud computing, IoT and autonomous machines. — [NGU news: Apply Online Now to the School of Engineering](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-news-events/apply-online-now-to-the-school-of-engineering/)
- The School of Engineering was established by Ministerial Decree "1951" issued in June 2021. NGU's site states its schools are recognised by the Ministry of Higher Education and the Supreme Council of Universities (SCU). — [NGU School of Engineering](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-academic/school-of-engineering/) (as summarised by search engine)
- UCL Engineering entered a **seven-year** education collaboration with NGU to deliver "two new, five-year degree courses": Architectural Engineering and Computers, Communications and Autonomous Systems. These form the foundation of NGU's new School of Engineering. — [UCL Consultants news](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/consultants/news/newgiza-university-ucl-collaborate-train-engineering-leaders-future)
- The degree is awarded by NGU, with curriculum support from UCL. It is **not** a UCL degree. — [NGU School of Engineering](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-academic/school-of-engineering/); [NGU news](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-news-events/apply-online-now-to-the-school-of-engineering/)
- An NGU announcement for AY 2023/2024 lists a 68% minimum Thanaweya Amma admission score for Engineering and describes it as an "8-semester" programme. — [NGU admissions/fees announcement](https://ngu.edu.eg/ng-news-events/newgiza-university-ngu-announces-the-minimum-admissions-requirements-for-its-schools-and-its-tuition-fees/)
- Engineering admission for certificate holders: 8 IGCSEs including Chemistry, Physics, English and Math, plus 1 AS or A-Level Math. English requirement is IELTS 6.0 / PTE 46 / TOEFL iBT 60. This is a third-party listing and may be outdated. — [theigclub NGU](https://theigclub.com/ngu/); [theigclub listing](https://theigclub.com/listings/new-giza-university/)
- NGU (Newgiza University) is a private university in 6th of October / Giza and admitted its first students in 2016. — [Wikipedia: New Giza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Giza_University)
- NGU's transfer-applicant checklist asks for the "previous university official original authenticated transcript" and the "grading scale". — [NGU Fall 2023 acknowledgement letter PDF](https://ngu.edu.eg/wp-content/uploads/Fall2023AckLetter.pdf)

### Inferences
- The sources conflict on programme length: UCL says five years, NGU's fee notice says 8 semesters. One possible explanation is a preparatory/foundation year plus 8 semesters, but this is not confirmed. If CCAS is a 5-year programme, ~84 credits is roughly half of a typical Egyptian engineering total (~160–180 credit hours in comparable private programmes). By credits, the student would then be closer to the midpoint than to the end of the degree.
- The CCAS name is non-standard. A receiving university will map it most naturally to Electrical/Electronic, Communications, Computer or Mechatronics/Robotics Engineering. Credits for maths, physics, programming and circuits should transfer most easily.
- The "UCL collaboration" does not confer a UK credential. Foreign universities will treat NGU credits as Egyptian private-university credits.

### Gaps
- **Total credit hours to graduate and the year-by-year course list for CCAS: Not confirmed.** No indexed public study plan or bylaw (اللائحة الداخلية) was found. To verify, request the CCAS programme bylaws/study plan from the NGU Registrar or School of Engineering, or check the student portal. The student should also get an official transcript plus course descriptions (توصيف المقررات), which every receiving institution will ask for.
- Whether CCAS specifically (not just the school) has a SCU programme-level approval and an Engineers-Syndicate–recognised specialisation name: Not confirmed. Ask the NGU School of Engineering for the SCU decree number for the CCAS bylaws.
- NAQAAE accreditation status of NGU engineering: not found.

## A2. NGU grading scale, probation/dismissal, graduation GPA, repeat policy, switching programmes

### Takeaway
NGU's own grading scale, D-grade point value, probation and dismissal thresholds, and repeat policy **could not be found in any public source**. What follows is the general pattern in Egyptian credit-hour institutions, used as a comparator only. In that pattern, D is the lowest passing grade, probation is triggered below a 2.0 cumulative GPA, and graduation requires a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0.

### Cited Findings
- NGU-specific results: none. Searches for NGU grading, probation and handbook returned no NGU regulation pages. — (searches run 2026-10-10; see Gaps)
- Comparator (New Mansoura University, an Egyptian *ahliya* university, NOT NGU): academic probation is triggered when the cumulative GPA at any main semester falls under 2.0. Degree eligibility requires a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. A course must earn at least a D to count as passed. — [NMU Evaluation System](https://www.nmu.edu.eg/en/students/evaluation-system)
- Comparator (Pyramids Higher Institute for Engineering and Technology, NOT NGU): the bachelor's requires at least 172 credit hours and passing all courses with a GPA of no less than 2.0 out of 4.0. — [Wikipedia: Pyramids Higher Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramids_Higher_Institute_for_Engineering_and_Technology)
- A generic summary gives D as roughly 1.0–2.0 points in Egyptian scales and says graduation requires a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. This is a weak, unverified source. — [Grokipedia: Academic grading in Egypt](https://grokipedia.com/page/academic_grading_in_egypt); see also [Scholaro: Egypt grading](https://www.scholaro.com/db/Countries/Egypt/Grading-System)

### Inferences
- With a CGPA of ~1.8–1.9, the student is very likely on academic probation at NGU now. Under the common Egyptian rule, they cannot graduate from NGU until the CGPA reaches 2.0. Credits earned with D grades probably count as "passed/earned", but they pull the CGPA down.
- **Attempted vs earned credits:** under standard credit-hour rules, F and W credits are attempted but not earned, and an F is usually included in the GPA until it is replaced by a repeat. The "~84 credits" figure must be split into earned (passed, D or better) and attempted. Foreign schools and Egyptian transfer rules count only passed courses, and often only those at 60%+ or C or better.
- Raising a 1.85 CGPA to 2.0 is arithmetically feasible. For example, at 84 attempted credits with CGPA 1.85, about 18 more credits at a 2.55 average lifts the CGPA to 2.0. Repeating D/F courses (if NGU's repeat policy replaces the grade) is faster still. Treat this as an illustration only, because it depends on NGU's repeat and grade-replacement rules.

### Gaps
- NGU's letter-grade table (A … D/D-/F, points, % bands), whether D- exists, the minimum pass grade, probation/dismissal thresholds (number of probation semesters before dismissal), repeat/grade-replacement policy, maximum years of study, and internal transfer between NGU programmes are **all Not confirmed**. To verify, get the NGU Student Handbook / Academic Regulations from the Registrar or student portal, or email the Office of Student Affairs. The student should also ask for an official "status statement" (بيان حالة) showing earned credits, attempted credits and CGPA, and for a formal note on whether they are on probation or at risk of dismissal. That status matters for both domestic transfer and many foreign admissions.

## B1. Transfer between Egyptian private (and ahliya) universities

### Takeaway
Current Egyptian transfer rules, as reported for AY 2025/2026, require a **cumulative GPA of at least 2.0** and **at least 60% in every course transferred**. They also require no disciplinary penalty or dismissal, and transferred credits are capped at **50% of the graduation requirement**. The student must still have at least **3 study years remaining** for programmes longer than 4 years (2 years for 4-year programmes). With a CGPA of 1.8–1.9 and mostly D grades, the student **does not currently qualify** to transfer to another Egyptian private/ahliya engineering faculty.

### Cited Findings
- 2025/26 rules reported from the Minister of Higher Education:
  - the student must meet the minimum Thanaweya Amma score for the target faculty's sector in the year they obtained the certificate;
  - the student must have completed one full academic year at the sending university;
  - **no course below 60%**;
  - **cumulative GPA not less than 2.0**;
  - no disciplinary penalties and no dismissal from the previous university;
  - the remaining study at the new faculty must be at least 3 years for faculties longer than 4 years, and 2 years for 4-year faculties;
  - the sending university must be recognised by the MoHE/SCU.

  — [Mobtada: Minister reviews transfer rules between private and ahliya universities](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818) (also [print version](https://www.mobtada.com/print/1084818)); [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/4756574)
- Council of Private (and Ahliya) Universities rules (2021/22-era decision, repeated in later coverage):
  - transferred credit hours studied outside the receiving university must not exceed **50%** of the credits needed to graduate;
  - every transferred course must be at least 60%;
  - the minimum CGPA for accepting transfers is set by each university's internal bylaws;
  - transfers are capped at 10% of the faculty's annual intake;
  - no disciplinary penalties or dismissal.

  — [Ahram Gate: Transfer between private universities](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2950190.aspx); [Ntalm-Masry](https://ntalm-masry.com/12420/); [Filgamaa](https://filgamaa.com/%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%b1%d9%81-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d8%b6%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%a8%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ad%d9%88%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a8%d9%8a%d9%86-%d8%a7/)
- Credit recognition works through an academic "clearing" (مقاصة علمية) that compares course descriptions and sets the remaining credits/years and the exempted courses. A certified full grade statement from the sending university is required. — [Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818); [CNU/MoHE ahliya transfer rules](https://cnu.mohesr.gov.eg/acceptance/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9)
- New Mansoura University (ahliya) states that no more than 50% of total graduation credits may be transferred. — [NMU transfer rules](https://www.nmu.edu.eg/ar/students/transfer-rules)
- Students transferring into an ahliya university from universities outside Egypt need transcripts authenticated by the foreign ministry of the host country and the Egyptian embassy there. — [CNU/MoHE](https://cnu.mohesr.gov.eg/acceptance/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9)

### Inferences
- **Domestic transfer is blocked for this student right now** on two counts: CGPA below 2.0, and D grades that are likely below 60%. Even after the CGPA is fixed, only courses at 60% or above transfer.
- The "3 remaining years" rule for 5-year engineering faculties means a year-3 transfer into another Egyptian private engineering faculty would effectively restart at year 2 or 3. Domestic transfer therefore does **not** shorten time to degree.
- Whether a D at NGU equals ≥60% depends on NGU's percentage bands, which are not confirmed. At many Egyptian credit-hour schools D or D+ falls in the 60–69% range, but this must be checked.

### Gaps
- The official text of the current (2026/27) Council of Private Universities transfer decision was not retrieved. Verify with the target university's admissions office or the MoHE/Council of Private Universities.
- Whether NGU itself permits internal programme switching at a CGPA below 2.0: Not confirmed.

## B2. SCU equivalency of foreign engineering bachelor's degrees (معادلة الدرجات الأجنبية)

### Takeaway
The SCU is the **only** Egyptian body that equates foreign degrees. It equates them **case by case, after graduation and return to Egypt**, against the corresponding Egyptian degree. Reported conditions include:
- **full-time, regular (in-person) study** is required, and online study should be avoided;
- the foreign university must be recognised or accredited by the home country's higher-education ministry;
- the student must comply with SCU rules for studying abroad, including a Thanaweya Amma score no more than 5% below the private/ahliya cutoff for the same faculty in the year of the certificate, and registration with the Missions administration;
- for some countries only listed universities are equated (Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia, Kazakhstan, Russia for students enrolled from 2019/20);
- Northern Cyprus degrees are **not** recognised (since 2019).

No published SCU rule was found on a minimum share of the degree studied at the awarding institution, on top-up degrees, or on engineering-technology degrees.

### Cited Findings
- The SCU is the sole Egyptian authority for equating foreign bachelor's, master's and PhD degrees. Equivalency is decided case by case after the degree is obtained, studies are completed and the student returns to Egypt, by comparison with the corresponding Egyptian degree. — [SCU General Administration for Degree Equivalency](https://scu.eg/en/administrations/general-administration-for-academic-degree-equivalency/); [SCU e-equivalency system guidelines](https://equ.scu.eg/GuideLines.aspx); [Masrawy 2026-04-18](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/4/18/2974152/%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%81%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D9%885-%D8%AD%D8%AF-%D8%A7-%D8%A3%D9%82%D8%B5%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%88%D9%84)
- **April 2026 SCU rules:**

  - certificates of those studying abroad are rejected if their Thanaweya score is more than 5% below the private/ahliya cutoff for the same faculty in the year they obtained the certificate.

- Required documents: an authenticated bachelor's certificate and an authenticated transcript/course statement. - Whether online or distance degrees can be equated depends on SCU controls, the study mode and the recognition of the awarding university. This is a secondary commercial-service source. — [iEgypt](https://iegypt.net/equivalency-of-certificates/)
- **Duration signal (UK-linked degree):** SCU Decree No. 151 (29/05/2023) equated the Coventry University Egypt-branch BEng degrees in Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical & Electronic Engineering for students who completed **240 ECTS**. ### Inferences
- **Online, distance and open-learning engineering degrees** (including most "fast online credit-transfer" options) are very likely **not equatable**. They fail the full-time regular-attendance condition reported in April 2026.
- **Northern Cyprus is eliminated** as a destination if Egyptian recognition matters. **Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia, Kazakhstan and Russia** are acceptable only at SCU-listed universities. Any destination must be accredited by its home ministry.
- The 240-ECTS condition in the Coventry decision suggests the SCU expects a **4-year (240 ECTS) engineering bachelor's**, not a 3-year 180-ECTS UK BEng. A short 3-year UK BEng (Hons) or a 1-year "top-up" on a non-UK base could therefore be judged short of the Egyptian standard. Expect the SCU to ask for extra courses or to refuse. This is an inference from one branch-campus decision, not a published general rule.
- Because the SCU compares degrees "case by case" with the Egyptian equivalent, a degree awarded largely by credit transfer (e.g., 1–2 years at the awarding university) faces an unknown risk. No explicit residency-share rule was found, but the regular-attendance condition and the transcript review make it a real risk. The awarding university's transcript should clearly show the transferred NGU credits and the in-residence credits.
- The 5% Thanaweya-score rule applies to students travelling to study abroad. The student was admitted to NGU engineering (2023/24 minimum 68%), so they likely meet the private-university engineering threshold for their certificate year. This needs checking against the private-university engineering cutoff for that year.

### Gaps
- No official SCU text was found that explicitly regulates:
  - (a) students who **transfer mid-degree from an Egyptian university to a foreign one** (credit carried in, minimum years at the awarding university);
  - (b) **top-up** degrees;
  - (c) **engineering technology** (e.g., BEngTech/BSc Engineering Technology) vs engineering degrees;
  - (d) ranking thresholds.

  All four are **Not confirmed**. To verify, submit an inquiry via the SCU e-equivalency portal ([equ.scu.eg](https://equ.scu.eg/GuideLines.aspx)) or the SCU equivalency administration, or ask the MoHE Missions administration for a written pre-enrolment opinion on the specific target university and programme.
- The full April 2026 SCU decision text and the current SCU lists of recognised universities by country were not retrieved. Check scu.eg FAQs/announcements.
- Whether the student must register with the Missions administration before enrolling abroad as a transfer student: Not confirmed (it was reported as a condition for Ukraine/Georgia/Armenia lists).

## B3. Egyptian Engineers Syndicate (نقابة المهندسين) registration for foreign-degree holders

### Takeaway
Syndicate membership requires an engineering bachelor's degree from an Egyptian university, or a degree that the **SCU has certified as equivalent** to an Egyptian engineering bachelor's (Law 66/1974). For foreign graduates, the **SCU equivalency certificate is decisive**. The Syndicate has also been restrictive about non-faculty-of-engineering qualifications: it refuses Computers & Information (Computer Science) graduates and limits private engineering institutes to a list of 13 that hold NAQAAE quality accreditation. Syndicate leadership has also floated an entry exam.

- Documents for graduates of foreign universities:
  - original and copy of the bachelor's certificate, authenticated by the Egyptian Foreign Ministry;
  - authenticated course/subject statement;

  - authenticated secondary-school transcript;
  - application in person only, with no proxy allowed.

## B4. Foreign branch campuses in Egypt (TKH-Coventry, UEL, Hertfordshire/GAF, BUE, UFE, etc.)

### Takeaway
Foreign branch campuses operate under **Law 162/2018**. Their degrees are equated through specific SCU decisions. TKH-Coventry engineering degrees (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical & Electronic) received SCU equivalency (Decree 151, May 2023) for 240-ECTS completers. TKH reports it was the first international branch campus whose engineering graduates were registered with the Engineers Syndicate (Syndicate Decree No. 3/2024).

### Cited Findings
- Law 162/2018 on establishing branches of foreign universities and university institutions in Egypt was published in the Official Gazette, issue 31 (bis A), on 2 August 2018. It created a Council for Affairs of Foreign University Branches. Each branch's establishing decree lists the degrees it may award, and "university institutions" (المؤسسات الجامعية) themselves do not award degrees. — [LinkedIn summary of Law 162/2018](https://ae.linkedin.com/pulse/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-162-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2018-%D9%81%D9%89-%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AE%D9%84-saad-eldin); [Dr Amin Lotfy office](https://draminlotfyoffice.com/details/11057)
- The SCU issued a decision equating Coventry branch certificates under the foreign-branches law. Study is administered via The Knowledge Hub Universities and students receive a UK parent-university degree. — [Elwatan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/6631799)
- SCU Decree No. 151 (29/05/2023) equates Coventry-Egypt BEng Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical & Electronic degrees for 240-ECTS completers. Students with only three years of Engineering are offered additional courses to meet SCU requirements. TKH says it took about two years of work with the SCU. — [TKH](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-degree-equivalency-egypt/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/breaking-news-coventry-university-branch-egypt-attains-degree-equivalency)
- The Engineers Syndicate approved TKH-Coventry engineering programmes by "Decree No. 3 / 2024", and Coventry-branch graduates were registered. TKH calls itself the first IBC registered with the Syndicate. — [TKH: graduates registered](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-university-branch-engineering-graduates-officially-registered-at-the-egyptian-engineering-syndicate/); [TKH: programmes approved](https://tkh.edu.eg/tkh-coventry-engineering-programmes-earn-egyptian-engineers-syndicate-accreditation/)
- BUE is described as an Egyptian private university, distinct from a branch campus. — [Wikipedia: BUE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_British_University_in_Egypt)

### Inferences
- **TKH-Coventry engineering is the best-documented "UK degree in Egypt" that is both SCU-equated and Syndicate-registrable.** It is a candidate destination for credit transfer, subject to its own transfer and GPA entry rules, which are not researched here. It also shows that even for a branch campus, the SCU insisted on a 240-ECTS / 4-year engineering degree.
- Other branches (UEL, Hertfordshire at GAF, Université Française d'Égypte, etc.) need programme-specific SCU equivalency and Syndicate approval. No evidence was found for their engineering programmes in this search.

### Gaps
- SCU equivalency and Syndicate status for engineering at UEL (TKH), University of Hertfordshire (GAF), UFE and other branches: Not confirmed. Check each institution's announcements and the SCU and Syndicate, and ask whether the specific engineering programme (not only the institution) holds both.
- All Coventry claims come from TKH's own website plus one Elwatan report. The official SCU or Syndicate decree texts were not retrieved.

## B5. Degrees obtained largely by credit transfer / "credit-transfer schemes"

### Takeaway
No Egyptian rule was found that explicitly addresses foreign degrees earned mostly through transferred credit. Domestic rules cap transferred credit at 50% of the degree and require at least 3 years of remaining study in 5-year faculties. The SCU equates "case by case" against an Egyptian equivalent and requires full-time regular study. A degree that is mostly transfer plus a short stay should therefore be treated as carrying meaningful equivalency risk.

### Cited Findings
- Domestic cap: transferred credits must not exceed 50% of graduation credits, and remaining study must be ≥3 years for faculties longer than 4 years. — [Ahram Gate 2950190](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2950190.aspx); [Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818); [NMU](https://www.nmu.edu.eg/ar/students/transfer-rules)
- SCU equivalency: case by case after graduation, compared with the Egyptian degree, with full-time regular study required. — [SCU equivalency administration](https://scu.eg/en/administrations/general-administration-for-academic-degree-equivalency/); [Youm7 2026-04-18](https://www.youm7.com/amp/2026/4/18/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B6-%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%A8%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-5/7381587)

### Inferences
- **A conservative design is safest for Egyptian recognition:**
  - ≥50% of the degree completed in residence, full-time and on-campus, at a home-ministry-recognised university with an accredited engineering programme;
  - a 4-year (≥240 ECTS / ~130+ US credits) "Engineering" bachelor's;
  - a country and university on any SCU list that applies.

  This mirrors the Egyptian domestic 50% rule. It is a risk-reduction heuristic, not a confirmed SCU rule.
- **Destinations and options likely eliminated if Egyptian recognition or the Syndicate matters:**
  - Northern Cyprus;
  - online, distance or open-university engineering degrees;
  - unlisted universities in Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia, Kazakhstan and Russia;
  - "Engineering Technology" or Computing degrees (Syndicate risk);
  - 3-year 180-ECTS degrees and top-ups (SCU 240-ECTS signal).
- **Pathways the evidence favours:** full-time on-campus 4-year accredited engineering bachelor's degrees abroad, or SCU-equated, Syndicate-approved branch-campus engineering in Egypt (e.g., TKH-Coventry).
- **The student's GPA below 2.0 is a hard blocker for domestic transfer.** It will also weigh on foreign admissions. Raising the NGU CGPA to ≥2.0 (one semester of repeats or strong grades) may unlock both domestic transfer and better foreign options.

### Gaps
- No SCU text on a minimum residency share or on transfer-heavy foreign degrees: Not confirmed. Request a written pre-enrolment opinion from the SCU equivalency administration or the MoHE Missions administration for the exact target university and programme before committing.
