# Transfer Options in Egypt, the Middle East, Turkey and Cyprus (North and South) for an NGU CCAS Student with Mostly D Grades and a CGPA of About 1.8–1.9

> **Method note (read first):** These notes were compiled on 2026-10-10. The research environment's network proxy **blocked direct page fetches** of almost every university domain (aast.edu, neu.edu.tr, emu.edu.tr, miuegypt.edu.eg, fue.edu.eg, khas.edu.tr, Egyptian news sites). Every finding below therefore comes from **search-engine extracts of the cited pages**, not from a full read of each page. Quoted figures carry the cited URL, but the report writer should treat them as "found in a search extract of the official page." Each one needs a final check against the live page or in writing with the institution. No requirement, fee or limit here was invented. Anything not found is marked **Not confirmed**.
>
> **Labels used:** **[Policy]** = supported by published policy; **[Estimate]** = preliminary estimate (reasoning, not a published rule); **[Unknown]** = requires written confirmation.

## Q1. Egypt: national rules for transfers between private universities, and the Egyptian candidates (AASTMT, MIU, MSA, FUE, Coventry/TKH, higher institutes)

### Takeaway
Inside Egypt, the binding national rules (as reported in 2021) set a **60% minimum for each transferred course**. They also cap transferred credits at **50% of graduation credits**, require **at least 3 academic years at the receiving college for programs longer than 4 years** (engineering), and require the Council of Private and Ahleya Universities to approve the clearance ("maqassa"). If NGU's D grade is 60% or more, D courses may be eligible in principle, but each university also applies its own GPA floor and course-match review. AASTMT explicitly requires **at least a C**, so mostly-D credits largely would not transfer there. The Coventry branch at The Knowledge Hub is the only foreign-branch engineering program confirmed as **approved by the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate**, but no advanced-entry or RPL policy was found for it.

### Cited Findings

**Egyptian national rules (Council of Private and Ahleya Universities / Ministry of Higher Education), as reported in the press in 2021. Not confirmed as unchanged in 2026:**
- **[Policy]** The percentage grade in any course transferred must be at least **60%** ("ألا تقل النسبة المئوية لأى من المقررات التى يتم نقلها للطالب عن 60%"). — [Amwal Al Ghad, 9 Sep 2021](https://amwalalghad.com/2021/09/09/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A-8-%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7/); [Mobtada](https://www.mobtada.com/details/1084818)
- **[Policy]** The minimum CGPA for accepting a transfer is set by **each university's internal bylaws**, so there is no single national GPA floor. — same sources as above
- **[Policy]** The student must have completed **at least one academic year**. Study at the receiving college must last **at least 3 academic years for colleges whose program exceeds 4 years**, and at least 2 years for 4-year colleges. — same sources as above
- **[Policy]** Transferred credit hours may not exceed **50% of the credit hours required for graduation**. — same sources as above
- **[Policy]** Students with disciplinary penalties or dismissal are not accepted. The sending university must be recognized by the Ministry of Higher Education and the SCU. Transfer papers are accepted only after the maqassa is done and approved by the university president, with approval from the Council of Private and Ahleya Universities. — same sources as above; also [Albawabh News](https://www.albawabhnews.com/4428064)
- **[Policy]** Students transferring **from foreign universities** need a legalized transcript and course content (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the host country plus the Egyptian embassy), a travel-movement certificate, and at least one full academic year of residence in that country. — search extract of the same rule set ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://amwalalghad.com/ui6h)). This matters only if he later transfers back to Egypt from abroad.

**AASTMT (Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport), College of Engineering & Technology, multiple branches:**
- **[Policy]** Transfer is open to students who completed courses at an accredited university in Egypt or abroad. They submit an official transcript and course descriptions to the Deanery of Admissions and Registration. — [AASTMT Admission Requirements](https://aast.edu/en/landing-page/admission.html)
- **[Policy]** The department "may consider transferring courses in which the applicant have scored **at least C** … after reviewing the course content." — [AASTMT Admission Requirements](https://aast.edu/en/landing-page/admission.html)
- **[Policy]** "Students must study **50% at least** of the total number of credits required for graduation at AASTMT." Transferred courses are listed as **TR** and **do not affect GPA**. "The admission decision is totally separate from transfer of courses." — [AASTMT Admission Requirements](https://aast.edu/en/landing-page/admission.html)
- Annual engineering fees are reported at roughly **US$6,000–7,000** (Alexandria, 2026). This is a third-party figure, and no official per-credit price was found. — [Egypt Telegraph](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/267792/)

**Misr International University (MIU):**
- **[Policy]** Transfer applicants submit an official enrollment statement and transcript. "Acceptance will be according to grades attained in previous university and places available at MIU." The number of transfer credits, quality of record, type of courses and **consistency of grades** are considered. Students moving to a different major "will be registered as freshman students regardless of the number of credit hours transferred." — [MIU Transfer Students](https://www.miuegypt.edu.eg/admission-requirements/general-admission-requirments/transfer-students/)
- **[Policy]** Graduation requires a CGPA of at least 2.00 and a major GPA of at least 2.00. — [MIU Student Guide 2023-24](https://www.miuegypt.edu.eg/wp-content/uploads/MIU-Student-Guide-2023-2024-V01.pdf) (via search extract)
- No undergraduate minimum transfer grade or GPA was published. (The B- rule found applies only to the master's program.) — [MIU MSc Electronics page](https://www.miuegypt.edu.eg/graduate-studies/masters-degree/electronics/)

**October University for Modern Sciences and Arts (MSA):**
- **[Policy]** MSA "welcomes all transfer students from government or privately owned universities." The faculty board reviews the transcript GPA, and the only published GPA minimum is for Dentistry (3–3.5). Applicants need a stamped course description for each subject and pay an **EGP 500 course-equivalency fee**. — [MSA Transfer Students](https://www.msa.eun.EG/msauniversity/admission/new-applicants/transfer-student); [archive copy](https://archive.msa.edu.eg/transfer-student)
- **[Policy]** Modules transferred from other universities are excluded from the MSA GPA. — [MSA Assessment & Progression](https://archive.msa.edu.eg/admission/assessment-progression)
- No engineering minimum transfer grade was found. Admissions hotline: **16672** (from the search extract). Email: Not confirmed.

**Future University in Egypt (FUE):**
- **[Policy]** Transfer students must meet FUE admission requirements, including a **high-school certificate score that complies with the Supreme Council of Private Universities' regulations for the year it was obtained**. Courses must be "substantially the same in both content and quality," and the transfer is subject to Ministry of Higher Education approval. Students who change major are registered as freshmen. Graduation requires a CGPA of at least 2.0. — [FUE Requirements for Transfer Students](https://fue.edu.eg/admissions/undergraduate_applicants/requirements_for_transfer_students); [FUE Admission Requirements](https://fue.edu.eg/admissions/undergraduate_applicants/admission_requirements)
- No minimum transfer GPA or minimum course grade was published.

**Coventry University branch at The Knowledge Hub (TKH), New Administrative Capital:**
- **[Policy]** The engineering programmes of the Coventry branch "have acquired approval from the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate" (Syndicate Decree No. 3 of 2024). Graduates receive a Coventry University award. — [TKH](https://tkh.edu.eg/?p=1147) / [TKH FAQ](https://tkh.edu.eg/faq/) (search extract)
- Year 1 is a foundation year, and students progress into the first discipline year (called "Year 2" at the Hub). — [TKH FAQ](https://tkh.edu.eg/faq/)
- No advanced-entry, direct-entry or RPL (recognition of prior learning) rule for engineering transfers was found. — [Coventry/TKH pages](https://www.coventry.ac.uk/theknowledgehub)

**Private Higher Institutes of Engineering (e.g., El Shorouk Academy):**
- The Higher Institute of Engineering at El Shorouk (founded 1995) awards a bachelor's degree "equated by the Supreme Council of Universities." Its departments include Communications Engineering and Electrical Power. — [Dirasa Abroad](https://www.dirasaabroad.com/shorouk-academy/) (third-party)
- The Engineers Syndicate has stated it will not register graduates of engineering institutes that lack the quality-assurance (NAQAAE) certificate. — [Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/6260067)
- No published rule was found for transferring **from a private university into a private higher institute**. The only institute-transfer rules found cover transfers between higher and middle institutes. They require meeting the receiving institute's announced minimum, the set percentage, capacity and score priority. — [Egypt Telegraph](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/69832)

**Egyptian Engineers Syndicate registration of foreign degrees:**
- Holders of a bachelor's degree from outside Egypt must submit the original degree legalized by the Egyptian MFA. They also need an **equivalency certificate from the Supreme Council of Universities (issued via the equivalency office at Cairo University)** and a legalized transcript. — [Filgamaa guide](https://filgamaa.com/%d9%83%d9%8a%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%82%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%85-%d9%84%d9%86%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%a8%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%87%d9%86%d8%af%d8%b3%d9%8a%d9%86-2025-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d9%88/) (secondary source)
- Equivalency generally requires that the specialization exists in Egyptian universities, that the study duration matches Egyptian norms, and that study was **full-time regular attendance**. Complementary courses may be required. — [Edugate](https://edugateuae.com/ar/blog-detail/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A) (secondary source)
- A mandatory training period of at least **3 months** was reported for graduates of private and foreign universities. — [Filgamaa](https://filgamaa.com/%d9%86%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%a8%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%87%d9%86%d8%af%d8%b3%d9%8a%d9%86-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%b1%d9%83-%d9%84%d9%88%d9%82%d9%81-%d9%82%d9%8a%d8%af-%d8%ba%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85/) (secondary source)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate]** Because Egyptian engineering is a 5-year program, the national rule of "at least 3 academic years at the receiving college for programs longer than 4 years" means a transfer to **any Egyptian private university likely leaves at least 6 semesters (3 years)**, whatever his 84 credits. The 50% cap would allow roughly 80–90 credits on a typical 160–180-credit plan, but the time floor dominates. This is a major time cost and needs written confirmation that the rule still applies in 2026.
- **[Estimate]** Whether his D grades meet the national 60% floor depends on **NGU's percentage-to-letter scale**, which was not found. If NGU's D band starts at or above 60%, D courses are eligible in principle. If D includes marks below 60%, they are not. **Action:** obtain NGU's official grading scale and a transcript showing percentages.
- **[Estimate]** AASTMT will likely accept only his C-or-better courses. With "mostly D" grades, very few of his 84 credits would transfer, so AASTMT is weak on priority #2. Its admission decision is separate from credit transfer, so admission itself might still be possible.
- **[Estimate]** MIU, MSA and FUE all review GPA case by case and publish no floor. A CGPA below 2.0 is a risk, and so is FUE's rule that the high-school score must meet the private-university minimum for its year.
- **[Estimate]** For Syndicate registration, Egyptian private universities and higher institutes with NAQAAE accreditation are the lowest-risk option. Coventry/TKH is confirmed as approved, but a transfer path into it is unproven.

### Gaps
- **Not confirmed:** whether the 2021 Council rules (60%, 50%, 3-year residency) are unchanged in 2026. *Verify:* Council of Private Universities circulars or each university's registrar; scu.eg.
- **Not confirmed:** NGU's grade scale (percentage band for D) and probation and dismissal rules (search found nothing official).
- **Not confirmed:** transfer rules for Nile University, Egyptian Russian University, Sinai, Badr, King Salman International University, the BUE, Hertfordshire at GAF and UEL at TKH. These could not be fetched or searched within budget.
- **Not confirmed:** whether private Higher Institutes of Engineering accept transfers from private universities, and under which maqassa rule. *Verify:* call or visit the student-affairs offices of El Shorouk Academy, Thebes and Modern Academy.
- **Not confirmed:** transfer-office emails for AASTMT, MIU, MSA and FUE. The only contact found is the MSA hotline 16672.

## Q2. Northern Cyprus (TRNC): NEU, EMU, CIU, Final, Lefke. Transfer leniency, D grades, fees and recognition

### Takeaway
Among TRNC universities, **EMU publishes a transfer CGPA floor of 2.00/4.00**, which on paper excludes a student at 1.8–1.9. **NEU and CIU publish no transfer GPA floor** in the material found. At both, **D/DD is a passing grade**, and exemptions are decided by department and faculty boards based on course content. Tuition is low (roughly €3,000–3,300 per year after the standard scholarship). The decisive risk is **recognition**. No official Egyptian SCU decision recognizing TRNC degrees was found, and the Republic of Cyprus says these degrees are recognized by no country other than Turkey. Engineers Syndicate registration would require an SCU equivalency certificate.

### Cited Findings

**Near East University (NEU), Nicosia:**
- **[Policy]** Transfers to NEU are possible from institutions offering similar programs. They are "subject to faculty board decisions," carried out "according to achievement or academic success levels and quotas determined in advance." Transfers from open universities are not possible. — [NEU Undergraduate Transfer Regulation (14.12.2021)](https://neu.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/14/undergraduate-transfer-14.12.2021.pdf); [NEU regulation of student admission affairs](https://neu.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/14/regulation-of-student-admission-affairs-14.12.2021.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Required documents are the transcript plus course descriptions, application form, diploma and passport. Transfer students need adequate English, set by the Preparatory School exam or a sufficient certificate. — same sources
- **[Policy]** Exemption requires a course-content match under the Undergraduate Transfer Regulation. It is approved by the administrative board after review by the **Horizontal Transfer and Adaptation Commission**. — [NEU Recognition of Prior Learning](https://dentistry.neu.edu.tr/student/course-selection/recognition-of-prior-learning/?lang=en)
- **[Policy]** An NEU diploma supplement states graduation requires "a minimum CGPA of 2.00/4.00 and **no failing grades (below DD)**," which confirms **DD is a passing grade** at NEU. — [NEU diploma supplement example](https://iletisim.neu.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/sites/117/2026/02/13/gazetecilik_diploma_eki.pdf)
- The governing academic regulation is dated 15.12.2025. — [NEU Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Studies 15.12.2025](https://neu.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/15/NEU-Academic-Regulations-For-Undergraduate-Studies-15.12.2025.pdf)
- No minimum transfer GPA and no minimum exemption grade were found in the search extracts. — [search across NEU documents above]
- Fees: undergraduate programs are listed at **€5,600 per year standard and about €3,225 with the 50% international scholarship (2026–27)**. This comes from a third-party agent's fee sheet, not NEU. — [North Cyprus Education PDF](https://api.northcypruseducation.com/uploads/2c12052226aa30522f107577f68e8947.pdf); Mechatronics Engineering is listed at €5,600 per year — [Bachelorsportal](https://www.bachelorsportal.com/studies/284772/mechatronics-engineering.html)
- NEU offers Computer, Electrical & Electronic and Mechatronics Engineering in English. — [Bachelorsportal Computer Engineering](https://www.bachelorsportal.com/studies/39702/computer-engineering.html)

**Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU), Famagusta:**
- **[Policy]** For undergraduate transfer applicants, "CGPA should be **minimum 2.00 out of 4.00** or the equivalent." Applicants must not have been dismissed and must have completed required modules from previous classes. Another extract states they must not have retaken courses from earlier semesters. Entry is into the 2nd–5th (or 6th) semesters. — [EMU Registrar – Transfers](https://registrar.emu.edu.tr/en/students/transfers); [EMU Undergraduate Admission](https://www.emu.edu.tr/en/prospective-students/undergraduate/undergraduate-admission/1292)
- **[Policy]** At EMU, a repeated course's last grade replaces the earlier one in the GPA. — [EMU Student Handbook 2025](https://registrar.emu.edu.tr/Documents/portal/Ogrenci-el-kitabi-en-26-09-2025.pdf)
- Previously taken courses approved by the faculty board are recorded as "exempted courses" with their letter grades. No undergraduate minimum exemption grade was found. (The graduate rule is a B minimum.) — [EMU admission regulation](https://mevzuat.emu.edu.tr/5-1-1-Rules-Entrance_exam.htm); [EMU grad transfer](https://grad.emu.edu.tr/en/admission/transfer-application)
- **[Policy]** Fees: the 2025–26 schedule for **transfer and newly registered international students** in the Faculty of Engineering lists about **US$5,605 full tuition**, with lower figures under 70% and 75% scholarships, plus a one-time US$360 filing fee. For 2026–27, all international students receive an **80% tuition scholarship** (except Medicine and Dentistry). — [EMU 2025-26 international fees PDF](https://www.emu.edu.tr/media/assets/files/tuition-fees/2025-2026-newly-registered-international-tuition-fees.pdf); [EMU transfer applications 2026-27](https://www.emu.edu.tr/en/news/announcements/applications-for-2026-2027-academic-fall-semester-external-transfers-between-institutions/1208/pid/5019) (search extract; the exact scholarship mechanics need checking)

**Cyprus International University (CIU), Nicosia:**
- **[Policy]** Students transferring from another university receive a **"T" grade** for courses whose equivalence the Head of Department or School Director approves. A T grade cannot be given for courses the student must repeat. — CIU regulation (2020) via search extract of [CIU Admissions Rules and Regulations V7](https://srs.ciu.edu.tr/srs/img/CIU-Admissions-Rules-and-Regulations-V7.pdf)
- **[Policy]** CIU rules for the associate degree require "at least 'D' or 'S'" in required courses and a CGPA of at least 2.00. **D is a passing grade.** — [CIU regulations on the principles of exam](https://ciu.edu.tr/sites/default/files/2023-05/_ciu-regulations-on-the-principles-of-exam.pdf)
- General undergraduate admission requires a secondary certificate and English (TOEFL iBT 65 or IELTS 5.5, otherwise the CIU exam). — [CIU Admission Requirements](https://ciu.edu.tr/en/become-student/international/admission-requirements)
- For graduate lateral transfers, the minimum is B (3.00) or 76/100. **This applies to graduate study only.** — [CIU E010 Graduate guidelines](https://ciu.edu.tr/sites/default/files/2026-06/E010-Graduate-Students-Course-Registration-Guidelines.pdf)

**Final International University / European University of Lefke:**
- No transfer or exemption-grade policy was found in searches. **Not confirmed.**

**Recognition:**
- The Republic of Cyprus Ministry agency states the TRNC institutions and their qualifications "are not recognised by the Republic of Cyprus or, therefore, by any other country with the exception of Turkey." This is a politically interested source. — [highereducation.ac.cy](https://highereducation.ac.cy/index.php/en/spoudes-cyprus/paranoma-tk-uni)
- TRNC universities are accredited by YÖDAK, and many are recognized by Turkey's YÖK. — [North Cyprus Education](https://www.northcypruseducation.com/are-degrees-from-north-cyprus-recognized/) (commercial source)
- Arabic search found **no official SCU (Egypt) statement** recognizing NEU or other TRNC universities. Third-party agents claim recognition but cite no official source. — [Studyshoot NEU](https://studyshoot.com/university/near-east-university/); [Almaal](https://almaal.org/recognized-turkish-cyprus-universities)
- A parallel case: Morocco froze equivalency of foreign degrees in mid-2024, affecting about 3,500 students (as reported). This shows recognition risk can materialize suddenly. — [Al3omk](https://al3omk.com/1123288.html)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate]** On paper, NEU and CIU are the **most permissive for a GPA below 2.0** in this region. Neither publishes a transfer GPA floor, D/DD passes, and exemptions are content-based. Whether courses with **D grades are exempted** is **[Unknown]**. Many Turkish-system universities require CC or C for exemption (see Q3), so a D-grade exemption cannot be assumed.
- **[Estimate]** EMU's published 2.00 floor excludes him unless he raises his NGU CGPA first, or EMU grants an exception (not documented).
- **[Estimate]** If NEU or CIU exempted most of his 84 NGU credits (unknown), a 4-year, roughly 240-ECTS engineering degree might leave 3–5 semesters. With only C-or-better or very closely matched courses exempted, more likely 5–6 semesters remain. **Unknown – requires written preliminary evaluation.**
- **[Estimate]** For priority #5 (registrable with the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate), TRNC degrees carry the highest risk in this file. Registration requires an SCU equivalency certificate, and no official SCU recognition of TRNC universities was found.

### Gaps
- **Not confirmed:** NEU and CIU minimum exemption grade (DD/DC/CC). *Verify:* email the faculty's Horizontal Transfer and Adaptation Commission with the transcript and syllabi, and ask for a written pre-evaluation before paying.
- **Not confirmed:** official SCU (Egypt) position on TRNC degrees. *Verify:* SCU Foreign Equivalency Department (Cairo University) and the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate registration committee, in writing, before enrolling.
- **Not confirmed:** transfer-office emails. A January 2026 NEU announcement reportedly lists an admissions phone and email, but it could not be read. — [NEU transfer announcement](https://neu.edu.tr/transfer-applications-begin-at-near-east-university-ranked-among-the-top-500-universities-in-the-world/?lang=en)
- **Not confirmed:** ABET or MÜDEK accreditation of specific NEU, EMU or CIU EE/Computer programs (not searched within budget).

## Q3. Turkey (private "vakıf" universities): horizontal transfer from abroad, GPA rules and D-grade exemptions

### Takeaway
Turkey's YÖK framework applies the **domestic inter-institutional transfer success condition (GPA ≥ 60/100, applied as ≥ 2.29/4 at Istanbul Aydın)** to students transferring from foreign universities. It also requires the foreign institution and program to be recognized by YÖK. A CGPA of 1.8–1.9 is **ineligible for yatay geçiş**. Starting as a new international student and seeking exemptions is the only route, and published exemption rules (e.g., TED University) count only **CC and above**, so D grades are unlikely to transfer.

### Cited Findings
- **[Policy]** Under the YÖK transfer regulation, inter-institutional horizontal transfer requires a GPA of **at least 60 out of 100** for completed semesters. For transfers **from foreign higher-education institutions**, the inter-institutional success conditions of **Article 11(2)** apply. — university reproductions of the YÖK regulation: [Karatekin Univ. PDF](https://krtknadmn.karatekin.edu.tr/files/oidb/2021/YÜKSEKÖĞRETİM%20KURUMLARINDA%20ÖNLİSANS%20VE%20LİSANS%20DÜZEYİNDEKİ%20PROGRAMLAR%20ARASINDA%20GEÇİŞ.pdf); [Gedik Univ. foreign-transfer conditions](https://www.gedik.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/yatay-gecis-yurtdisi-madde14-kosullar.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Istanbul Aydın University (private): for foreign horizontal transfer, the GPA must be **≥ 60/100 or ≥ 2.29/4**. The foreign institution and its program must be **recognized by YÖK**. — [Istanbul Aydın Yurtdışı Yatay Geçiş Usul ve Esasları](https://www.aydin.edu.tr/tr-tr/ogrenciler/kayit-kabul/Documents/Yurtd%C4%B1%C5%9F%C4%B1%20Yatay%20Ge%C3%A7i%C5%9F%20Usul%20ve%20Esaslar%C4%B1.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Istanbul Aydın (inter-institutional): department names must be identical or content at least **80%** compatible. No transfer into the first or last semesters. — [Aydın Kurumlararası Yatay Geçiş](https://www.aydin.edu.tr/tr-tr/ogrenciler/kayit-kabul/Documents/Kurumlararas%C4%B1%20Yatay%20Ge%C3%A7i%C5%9F%20Usul%20ve%20Esaslar%C4%B1.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Kadir Has University (private): for foreign transfer, the sending university must be in the **top 400** of at least three of THE, QS, ARWU and CWTS Leiden. Otherwise placement-score conditions apply. At least four semesters must be passed. — [KHAS Yurt Dışı Yatay Geçiş Koşulları 2025](https://www.khas.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Yurt-Disi-Yatay-Gecis-Basvuru-Kosullari.pdf)
- **[Policy]** TED University (private) exemption directive (2025 version) counts courses completed with **CC or higher** and accepted by the faculty board. — [TEDU KYS-YN-50](https://www.tedu.edu.tr/sites/default/files/docs/KYS-YN-50_DersMuafiyetiveIntibakIslemleriYonergesi.pdf)
- Example of a state university practice: DC and DD are excluded and CC or above is needed to count a course. — [Ostim Technical University – Course Exemptions](https://oidb.ostimteknik.edu.tr/course-exemptions)
- Example of a state university practice (Ibn Haldun, private): minimum C for undergraduate exemption, plus at least 80% content similarity for compulsory courses. — [IHU](https://oidb.ihu.edu.tr/en/ders-saydirma-ve-muafiyet-islemleri)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate]** Turkey is effectively **closed via yatay geçiş** for a CGPA of 1.8–1.9 (below 2.29). A fresh international admission is possible in principle, but with CC/C exemption floors, his D courses would mostly not count. That means close to a full 4-year restart, which fails priorities #1–3.
- **[Estimate]** An exemption exception for an exceptional university with a DD floor cannot be ruled out, but none was found.

### Gaps
- **Not confirmed:** whether Altınbaş, Okan, Istanbul Aydın or Medipol grant exemptions for DD/DC grades to new international entrants. *Verify:* each university's "Muafiyet ve İntibak Yönergesi."
- **Not confirmed:** current 2026 text of the YÖK regulation (amended several times). *Verify:* Resmî Gazete / yok.gov.tr.

## Q4. Arab Middle East (UAE, Jordan, Gulf, Lebanon): transfer grade floors and GPA requirements

### Takeaway
Accredited private universities in the UAE consistently require a **C (2.0) minimum per transferred course**, typically a **transfer-admission CGPA of 2.0**, and **50–75% residency**. Jordanian universities require **C+ (68%)** at the University of Jordan and cap transfers at **50%**. None of these suits mostly-D grades with a CGPA below 2.0.

### Cited Findings
- **[Policy]** Ajman University: the previous course grade "must be **at least C** (2.0 on a 4.0 scale)." Credit hours must be no fewer than the AU course and content similar. The college transfer committee approves. **AU requires at least 75% of program credits at AU.** Transferred grades are excluded from the CGPA. — [Ajman University Undergraduate Transfers](https://ajman.ac.ae/en/admissions/undergraduate-transfers)
- **[Policy]** Abu Dhabi University: credits "must be at least at **C** grade or equivalent." — [ADU Transfer Students](https://www.adu.ac.ae/study/admissions/transfer-students)
- **[Policy]** Amity University Dubai: undergraduate transfer requires a **minimum CGPA of 2.0** and a **C** minimum per course. Transfer is capped at **50%** of program credits. — [Amity Dubai transfer policy PDF](https://amityuniversity.ae/storage/public_documents/AMUD-S24%20Transfer%20Admissions,%20Transfer%20Credit%20and%20Advanced%20Standing2024.11.12.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Al Ain University policy uses the same C threshold and 50% cap. — [AAU transfer policy PDF](https://api.aau.ac.ae/uploads/editor/source/policies/Staff_(Admin&Academic_Policies)/transfer-admissions-credit.pdf)
- **[Policy]** UAE University (public) accepts **C− or better** and requires 75% residency, including all senior major courses. — [UAEU PRO-AE-06](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/about/procedures/admissions_and_enrollment/pro-ae_06_en.pdf)
- **[Policy]** University of Jordan: transferred course grade must be at least **C+ (68%)**. Credit is capped at **50%** of the plan, and transferred grades are excluded from the GPA. — [UJ Credit Transfer PDF](https://registration.ju.edu.jo/en/english/Documents/Credit%20Transfer%20.pdf)
- **[Policy]** Middle East University (Jordan, private): at most **50%** of passed courses in the plan can be counted, and grades are not counted in the GPA. Grade threshold not found. — [MEU Transfer Students](https://www.meu.edu.jo/?p=11809)
- The sending university must be recognized by the Jordanian Ministry of Higher Education. — [JUST course equivalency](https://just.edu.jo/Admission/undergraduate/Pages/CORSEEQUIVARA.aspx)

### Inferences
- **[Estimate]** In the UAE and Jordan, essentially **none of his D-grade credits would transfer**, and a CGPA of 1.8–1.9 would fail the 2.0 transfer floor at least at Amity. These systems rank low for this student.
- **[Estimate]** The CAA (UAE) standard itself was not read. The consistency of C floors across institutions suggests C is the effective system norm.

### Gaps
- **Not researched (budget/network):** Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Oman and Lebanon. Given the regional pattern (Saudi engineering colleges reviewed require CGPA ≥ 2.75/4 for external transfer — [Imam University](https://units.imamu.edu.sa/colleges/en/Engineering/Pages/transfer_college.aspx)), they are unlikely to be more lenient, but this is **not confirmed**.
- **Not confirmed:** the text of the UAE CAA Standards 2019 on transfer credit (caa.ae could not be fetched).

## Q5. Republic of Cyprus (University of Nicosia, European University Cyprus, Frederick, Neapolis)

### Takeaway
The University of Nicosia has long accepted credit transfers from abroad, and transferred grades do not enter the UNIC CPA. However, **no minimum transfer grade or GPA was found** for UNIC, EUC, Frederick or Neapolis. Recognition is EU-standard (strong), but costs are higher and D-grade acceptance is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- UNIC states that transferring credits from universities abroad "has been in place since the University's inception." Students on undergraduate programs abroad can continue in Cyprus after courses corresponding to **at least 60 ECTS**. — [UNIC announcement](https://www.unic.ac.cy/meteggrafes-kyprion-foititon-panepistimion-toy-exoterikoy-sto-panepistimio-leykosias/)
- "Transfer credits and grades are not used in computing the University of Nicosia CPA." — [UNIC Transient Student Form](https://oldstudentintranet.unic.ac.cy/Portals/0/Forms/2017/TRANSIENT%20STUDENT%20FORM.pdf)
- European University Cyprus requires official transcripts and course descriptions for transfer. No grade floor was found. — [EUC](https://euc.ac.cy/en/?p=3233)
