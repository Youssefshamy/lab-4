# Engineering transfer / advanced standing: Australia, New Zealand and other less-obvious regions (Africa, Russia/Central Asia, Latin America) for a GPA < 2.0, mostly-D student

Student profile reminder: NGU (Egypt) CCAS major, about 84 US-style credit hours, mostly D grades, cumulative GPA about 1.8–1.9/4.0, some F/W/repeats.

Research method note (important for the report writer): searches ran on 2026-10-10. Direct page fetches to Australian university domains (cqu.edu.au, handbook.cqu.edu.au, unisq.edu.au, policy.federation.edu.au) and to immi.homeaffairs.gov.au **failed** with DNS or proxy 403 errors. So some figures below come from search-engine snippets of official pages and not from full-page reads. Third-party sources are labelled as such. Anything not confirmed is marked "Not confirmed" with how to check it.

## Q1. Australia: does a D (passing) grade earn credit, how much credit is possible, and what are the admission rules for GPA < 2.0?

### Takeaway
Australian universities usually give credit (advanced standing / RPL) only for completed units with a **"pass or higher"** grade. Whether an Egyptian/NGU "D" counts as a "pass" is decided case by case against the home institution's grading scale. I found no Australian university that publishes a rule saying "D from a US-style system = credit". Accredited 4-year BEng (Hons) degrees cap credit at about 50% (one example: JCU, 48 of 96 credit points) and require final-year/thesis units to be taken at the receiving university. The realistic result is roughly 1–2 years of credit, leaving about 2–3 years of study at about AUD 75k+/year all-in. Admission with a cumulative GPA < 2.0 through prior-university entry is unconfirmed. The likely route is admission on the Egyptian secondary certificate (Thanaweya Amma / IGCSE / American Diploma) as the "Year-12 equivalent", with credit assessed separately.

### Cited Findings
**General credit rules (minimum grade):**
- University of Adelaide credit procedure: prior study "must have been completed successfully with a pass grade or higher" to be considered for credit — [Adelaide Credit Procedure](https://adelaide.edu.au/about/policies/recognition-of-prior-learning-policy/credit-procedure/)
- Macquarie University RPL Policy: credit for formal prior learning is limited to completed units whose final grade is a pass or higher. External credit is recorded **without a grade** and does not affect the weighted average mark — [Macquarie RPL Policy](https://policies.mq.edu.au/document/view.php?id=151)
- Federation University HE RPL procedure: for overseas qualifications, credit is guided by the Course Coordinator. The minimum grade text was not retrieved because the page fetch failed — [Federation HE RPL Procedure](https://policy.federation.edu.au/academic_governance/policy/ch02.php)
- Victoria University (TAFE RPL page): overseas or private university transcripts must be provided "from the appropriate government body/professional association recognised in Australia". No minimum grade stated — [VU RPL](https://www.vu.edu.au/study-at-vu/courses/credit-for-skills-past-study/recognition-of-prior-learning-rpl)

**Credit caps for Engineers Australia-accredited BEng (Hons):**
- No Engineers Australia-wide credit cap was found in search. The caps are set by each university — (search result, no EA source found)
- James Cook University BEng (Hons) [Embedded], accredited by Engineers Australia: the maximum advanced standing is **48 of 96 credit points (50%)**. Credit only counts for study completed within 10 years. At least 18 credit points (including 6 cp of 4th-year thesis) must be taken at JCU at each of levels 3 and 4. This is from the 2015/2016 handbooks, so it may be outdated — [JCU 2016 handbook](https://www.jcu.edu.au/course-and-subject-handbook2/course-and-subject-handbook-2016/courses/undergraduate-courses/bachelor-of-engineering-honours-embedded); [JCU 2015 handbook](https://jcu.edu.au/course-and-subject-handbook-2015/course-information/undergraduate-courses/bachelor-of-engineering-honoursembedded)
- JCU 2010: credit near the maximum should generally come from the first two levels of the program — [JCU 2010 BEng](https://jcu.edu.au/course-and-subject-handbook-2010/course-information/undergraduate-courses/bachelor-of-engineering)

**CQUniversity (CQU): best-documented flexible candidate**
- International credit: assessed **case by case**, which can take **up to 6 weeks**. Credit is given only for units at diploma level or higher completed **within the last 10 years**. Work experience earns no credit. The application needs a certified transcript, a **unit outline for each unit**, and course details submitted via the iStart admission application. Credit must be **approved in writing before enrolment**. Overseas documents must be official and translated into English — [CQU International Credit Transfers](https://www.cqu.edu.au/study/international/credit-transfers) (search snippet; the page fetch failed)
- CQU states no minimum grade for credit in retrieved text. The only grade rule found is a "pass grade average" for **postgraduate** entry — [CQU Academic Requirements](https://cqu.edu.au/study/international/entry-requirements/academic-requirements) (search snippet)
- CQU BEng (Hons) (code CC31): 2026 international indicative first-year fee **AUD 44,910** (first term AUD 20,160), based on 24 credit points per term × 2 terms. Fees exclude visa, OSHC and living costs, and "may vary depending on … any credit awarded for RPL" — [CQU Handbook CC31](https://handbook.cqu.edu.au/he/courses/view/CC31); [CQU International courses and fees 2026 PDF](https://delivery-cqucontenthub.stylelabs.cloud/api/public/content/international-courses-and-fees-list-852713); [CQU course page (international)](https://cqu.edu.au/courses/bachelor-of-engineering-honours?show=international). TopUniversities' AUD 39,120 is the 2025 figure and is outdated — [TopUniversities](https://www.topuniversities.com/universities/central-queensland-university-cquniversity-australia/undergrad/bachelor-engineering-honours)
- CQU general credit/RPL page — [CQU Credit and RPL](https://www.cqu.edu.au/study/prepare/credit-for-prior-learning)
- CQU Associate Degree of Engineering: a pathway into the Bachelor of Engineering Technology. One listing says it is **part-time only** (likely not usable on a student visa). Course code listed as CC02. Majors include Electrical. It requires 240 hours of Engineering Professional Practice. Graduates meet the academic requirements for Engineers Australia membership as an "Engineering Associate". These are third-party listings — [Good Universities Guide](https://gooduniversitiesguide.com.au/course-provider/cquniversity-australia/associate-degree-in-engineering); [Prosple](https://be-1047.frontend.prosple.com/institutions/cquniversity-australia-cqu/courses/associate-degree-of-engineering); [QTAC](https://www.qtac.edu.au/directory-courses_old/listing/49694/)
- CQU Bachelor of Engineering Technology exists (Rockhampton campus) — [TopUniversities](https://topuniversities.com/universities/central-queensland-university-cquniversity-australia/undergrad/bachelor-engineering-technology)

**UniSQ:**
- International undergraduate (first-degree) entry is based on comparison with Australian Year 12 — [UniSQ entry requirements](https://www.unisq.edu.au/international/apply/entry-requirements)
- UniSQ has a "Credit and Exemption procedure". Its grade rule was **not retrieved** — [UniSQ (via PostgradAustralia listing)](https://postgradaustralia.com.au/institutions/university-of-southern-queensland-usq/courses/graduate-certificate-of-engineering-practice)

**Charles Darwin University (CDU):**
- Undergraduate entry is based on Year-12 equivalence. For **B Engineering**, the country table (e.g., Bangladesh) requires a minimum **GPA 4.5/5 in Maths, Physics or Chemistry** at secondary level. CDU pathway diplomas give 2nd-year entry for business/IT/computer science but **not engineering** — [CDU International Entry Requirements](https://www.cdu.edu.au/international/entry-requirements)

**Engineering Institute of Technology (EIT, Perth, private):**
- BSc (Electrical/Mechanical etc.), 3 years full-time, also delivered online. TEQSA-regulated. Reported as **provisionally accredited by Engineers Australia under the Sydney Accord** (technologist level, not Washington Accord). Has CRICOS registration. These are **aggregator sources only** — [educations.es EIT](https://www.educations.es/institutions/eit/on-campus-bachelor-of-science-electrical-engineering); [Australian Export Awards](https://www.exportawards.gov.au/stories/eit-empowers-the-engineers-of-the-future)

**Visa and living costs (Australia):**
- Student visa (subclass 500) charge: **AUD 2,500 from 1 July 2026** (was AUD 2,000). The sources are third-party and not Home Affairs — [Insider Guides](https://insiderguides.com.au/australia-has-increased-student-visa-fees-again-heres-what-international-students-need-to-know/); [IDP](https://www.idp.com/australia/study-to-migrate/australian-visa-fee-increases/). Check on the [Home Affairs Visa Pricing Estimator](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/visa-pricing-estimator)
- Financial capacity (living costs) for a single student: **AUD 29,710 for 12 months**, on top of first-year tuition and travel. Travel estimate from Africa: AUD 2,000 — [Navitas financial capacity guidelines](https://www.navitas.com/study/apply/genuine-student/financial-capacity/); [One Planet Migration Law 2026](https://oneplanetmigrationlaw.com.au/immigration-blog/student-visa-500-financial-capacity-requirement-2026/) (third-party)

### Inferences
- **Does D count?** Unknown – requires written confirmation. Australian policy wording is "pass or higher". An NGU D is a passing grade on NGU's transcript, so on the plain wording it should be eligible. However, assessors compare the content and level of each unit using unit outlines, and some may treat D as a marginal pass. Ask for a written credit assessment through the admission application before paying (CQU explicitly does this before enrolment).
- **Credit estimate at CQU BEng (Hons)** (192 cp total = 4 yrs × 48 cp, derived from the fee basis of 24 cp/term). Preliminary estimate: 48–72 cp of credit (1–1.5 years), mostly first-year maths, physics, programming and circuits units. If CQU applies a cap similar to JCU's 50%, the maximum is about 96 cp (2 years). That would need nearly all 84 NGU credits to map, which is unlikely given the Fs/Ws. **Remaining: about 5–6 terms (2.5–3 years); best case 4 terms.** Preliminary estimate.
- **Admission with GPA < 2.0:** CQU's credit page and the CDU/UniSQ pages base undergraduate admission on Year-12 equivalence, not on the university GPA. The student may therefore be admissible on their Egyptian secondary certificate even with a low university GPA. Unknown – requires written confirmation, because an application listing prior study may trigger an academic-standing review.
- **Total cost estimate (CQU, 2.5–3 years):** AUD 44,910 tuition + 29,710 living + OSHC (Not confirmed; check the OSHC providers' quote) + visa AUD 2,500 once + about AUD 2,000 travel. That is about **AUD 77k for the first year excluding OSHC**, and **about AUD 195k–235k in total**. Preliminary estimate. This is likely far more expensive than the European/Asian options covered elsewhere.
- **Degree type vs Egyptian Syndicate:** a BEng (Hons) under the Washington Accord is the only Australian award likely to be treated as an "engineering bachelor" by the SCU. BEngTech/BSc (Sydney Accord) and Associate Degree (Dublin Accord level) are technologist/associate qualifications and are **high risk** for Engineers Syndicate registration.
- **Easiest-courses factor:** CQU, UniSQ and Federation are regional, mass-access universities with wide ATAR ranges and online delivery. This is about access, not lower standards. No official "difficulty" data exists. Anecdotal evidence was not gathered because Reddit searches did not return relevant results within the time budget.

### Gaps
- CQU, Federation, VU, UniSQ, Swinburne, ECU, Murdoch, SCU and WSU: published minimum grade for credit, published **max credit for BEng (Hons)**, and credit precedent databases. Page fetches failed (DNS/403). Check by emailing the international admissions offices (CQU via iStart/international enquiries; UniSQ international admissions) with the NGU transcript, NGU grading scale and course descriptions, and ask for a **pre-application credit assessment**.
- Current Engineers Australia accreditation status of CQU CC31 majors (Electrical, Mechatronics?) was not verified. Check the [Engineers Australia accredited programs list](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/).
- OSHC 2026 premium was not found.
- Retake/academic progression rules (e.g., "show cause" after failing 50% of units) were not retrieved for any Australian university.
- No source on the Egyptian SCU's treatment of Australian BEng (Hons) for students who transferred from an Egyptian private university.

## Q2. New Zealand: BEngTech / BE (Hons) transfer options and costs

### Takeaway
New Zealand polytechnics (now regional institutes after the Te Pūkenga changes) offer a 3-year **Bachelor of Engineering Technology (BEngTech)** accredited by Engineering New Zealand under the **Sydney Accord**. Ara's international fee is **NZD 29,761/yr (2026)**. Credit rules for overseas university study exist at NZ universities (Level 5+ study, a 10-year recency limit at Waikato), but no polytechnic BEngTech credit cap or minimum grade was found. BEngTech is a technologist degree, so it is a high risk for Egyptian Engineers Syndicate registration.

### Cited Findings
- Ara Institute of Canterbury, BEngTech (Electrical) CH4018: 3 years. Electrical and mechanical majors are fully accredited by Engineering New Zealand and recognised under the **Sydney Accord**. International fee 2026 **NZD 29,761/yr** (2025: NZD 29,120), plus a student services fee of about NZD 392.50. Entry requires NCEA L3 with 14 credits each in Physics and Calculus (or equivalent). International close dates: 27 Jan (Feb start) / 23 Jun (Jul start, mechanical). Pages conflict on start dates (July 2026 vs Feb 2027) — [Ara BEngTech Electrical](https://www.ara.ac.nz/products/programme/ch4018-elec-bachelor-of-engineering-technology-electrical/); [Ara BEngTech Mechanical](https://www.ara.ac.nz/products/programme/ch4018-mech-bachelor-of-engineering-technology-mechanical)
- Victoria University of Wellington: credit transfer is possible for courses completed at diploma or degree level (**NZQA Level 5+**) in NZ or overseas. Overseas applications take longer — [VUW transfer credit](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/study/apply-enrol/transfer-credit)
- University of Waikato: credit may be refused for study completed more than 10 years ago — [Waikato credits and transfers](https://waikato.ac.nz/study/apply/credits-and-transfers)
- A sample NZ private-institution credit recognition and transfer policy (UCIC) — [UCIC policy PDF](https://www.ucic.ac.nz/wp-content/uploads/credit-recognition-transfer-policy1.pdf) (not engineering-specific)

### Inferences
- Ara BEngTech is 3 years (360 NZ credits). With credit for first-year maths, physics and circuits, perhaps 0.5–1 year could be credited, leaving **about 2–2.5 years**. Preliminary estimate. Living costs and the NZ student visa fee were not researched (Not confirmed; see Immigration NZ "Fee Paying Student Visa").
- Cost: about NZD 30k tuition plus living costs per year. This may be cheaper than Australia, but it leads to a **technologist** degree. Under the Egyptian Syndicate rule, a degree must be "SCU-certified equivalent to a bachelor's in Engineering", and a 3-year BEngTech is unlikely to pass that (Preliminary estimate). A NZ BE (Hons) at AUT/Canterbury (Washington Accord) would be needed for Syndicate safety. Those universities' entry and credit rules were not retrieved.

### Gaps
- Credit caps and minimum grade at Ara, Unitec, Wintec, Otago Polytechnic, AUT and Canterbury for overseas study were not found. Ask each institution's international office for a credit pre-assessment.
- Whether NZ institutions accept GPA < 2.0 for prior-university applicants is not confirmed.
- NZQA/Engineering NZ list of accredited BEngTech/BE (Hons) programmes was not fetched — [Engineering New Zealand](https://www.engineeringnz.org/).

## Q3. Other regions quick scan (sub-Saharan Africa, Russia/Belarus/Central Asia, Latin America)

### Takeaway
None of these regions produced a clearly viable, documented route. Kenya's national credit transfer framework historically requires a **cumulative GPA of 2.0** to transfer between universities, which blocks this student, though one Kenyan university accepts D grades for credit. Russian transfers follow Ministry Order No. 607 (2021), but these routes are almost entirely Russian-taught. A Kazakh university's foreign-student page sets GPA 3.0. Mauritius applies local entry rules with no transfer-credit policy found. Latin America was not researched further because of language (Spanish/Portuguese instruction). These regions should be ranked **low** for this profile.

### Cited Findings
**Kenya**
- Maasai Mara University Credit Transfer Policy: **minimum grade for credit transfer is D**. The maximum credit is **49% of core courses**, following CUE standards. This is a single university, and it has no engineering programme focus — [MMU Credit Transfer Policy PDF](https://www.mmarau.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Credit-Transfer-Policy-01.pdf)
- CUE (Commission for University Education) Credit Accumulation and Transfer System (older document): to transfer between universities, a student "must have attained at least a cumulative grade C or **GPA of 2.0** on a 1–4 scale". The student must take at least **52% of units** at the graduating university — [CUE CATS document](https://www.cue.or.ke/index.php?Itemid=496&download=108%3Abed-science-july-2012&id=17%3Acredit-accumulation-and-transfer-system&option=com_phocadownload&view=category)
- Kenya's national credit system KCATS dates from 2018 — [The Star explainer (2024)](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2024-03-12-explainer-what-you-need-to-know-about-credit-transfer-system)

**Mauritius**
- University of Mauritius: international students "must satisfy the same minimum entry requirements… as the local students". Fees are payable in MUR/USD/EUR. Lab fee for S&T programmes: Rs 5,250/yr (2018–19 regulations, may be outdated). A 50% block-fee scholarship is available for some international students recruited via approved agents or from specified regions — [UoM International UG Guidelines 2020](https://uom.ac.mu/images/FILES/Admissions/2020/International/Undergraduate/Guidelines.pdf); [UoM Regulations 2018–19 ch.17](https://www.uom.ac.mu/Images/Files/Regulations/2018_2019/Chap17.pdf)
- Middlesex University Mauritius campus (Quatre Bornes): a third-party profile from 2012 lists UG tuition at £10,400/yr (outdated). Whether BEng Electronic Engineering runs at the Mauritius campus is not confirmed — [CollegeTransfer profile](https://www.collegetransfer.org/MiddlesexUniversityInternational/TransferProfile/tabid/145/Default.aspx?radius=0)

**Rwanda / Ghana / South Africa**
- No transfer-credit policy for University of Rwanda, Ashesi or South African universities (ECSA/SAQA) was found within the search budget.

**Russia**
- Transfers between higher education organizations are governed by **Ministry of Science and Higher Education Order No. 607 of 12 July 2021**. One university says budget-funded transfer is allowed only if the total study duration does not grow by more than one academic year — [USMA transfer page](https://usma.ru/?p=72801)
- Applicants with education documents from outside Russia need a certificate of recognition of the foreign education (HSE) — [HSE transfer](https://hse.ru/en/studyspravka/transfer)
- Transfers of Russian-government-scholarship students require Ministry approval — [HSE International Student Support](https://istudents.hse.ru/en/transferhse)
- Older SPbU guidance (2014): transfer is possible only if the course difference is 10 or fewer courses — [SPbU guestbook](https://guestbook.spbu.ru/prorektory-spbgu/prorector-obr/8069-transfer-program-or-twinning-program.html)
- MPEI (Moscow Power Engineering Institute): bachelor's is 4 years and foreigners must show tuition, accommodation and insurance funds. ITMO's English-taught programmes are mainly master's, and bachelor's teaching is mostly in Russian — [MPEI bachelor](https://www.mpei.ru/lang/en/study-mpei/Pages/bachelor-course.aspx); [ITMO news](https://news.itmo.ru/en/news/5498/)
- Russian tuition about USD 5,000/yr cited for one Moscow State faculty (2023 journalism) — [Novaya Gazeta Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/amp/articles/2023/09/17/russia-s-brain-gain-en)

**Kazakhstan**
- Kazakh Economic University (KEU) foreign-student page: bachelor entry at courses 2–4 with a **minimum GPA 3.0** (this page appears to describe an exchange-type programme) — [KEU foreign students](https://keu.kz/en/international/foreign-students-en.html)

**Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Latin America**
- No specific sources were retrieved.

### Inferences
- **Kenya:** if the CUE 2.0 GPA rule still applies under KCATS, a GPA 1.8–1.9 student is **ineligible to transfer** (Supported by published policy, though the document is old). Kenyan engineering degrees also need Engineers Board of Kenya accreditation (Not confirmed).
- **Russia/Belarus:** transfers with "academic difference" exams are realistic only into **Russian-language** programmes, usually after a preparatory language year. That adds time, which conflicts with priorities 1 and 3. An Egyptian SCU equivalence for Russian engineering degrees is common in practice (Not confirmed – no source retrieved).
- **Mauritius:** UoM uses local entry rules, and no low-GPA transfer route is documented. Unknown – requires written confirmation.
- **Latin America:** excluded on language grounds. No sources searched.

### Gaps
- University of Rwanda, Ashesi (Ghana), Strathmore/JKUAT (Kenya), and South African CAT policies: not retrieved.
- Belarus (e.g., BSUIR) English-taught engineering and transfer rules: not retrieved.
- The full text of Russia's Order 607 on transfers from **foreign** institutions (vs between Russian ones) was not retrieved. Check on publication.pravo.gov.ru or with the target university's international office.

## Q4. Egyptian recognition (SCU equivalence / Engineers Syndicate): cross-cutting risk

### Takeaway
To join the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate, a graduate needs an engineering bachelor's from an Egyptian university **or** one that the Supreme Council of Universities (SCU) certifies as equivalent. Equivalence is not automatic registration. Technologist degrees (BEngTech, Sydney Accord BSc) and associate degrees are therefore high risk. The SCU's rules on degree duration and on students who transferred out of Egyptian universities were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Engineers Syndicate membership requires "a bachelor's degree in Engineering from an Egyptian university or a degree certified by the Supreme Council of Universities to be equivalent to a bachelor's degree in Engineering", plus nationality/reciprocity and good conduct conditions — [Nazra Q&A on Engineers Syndicate Law (PDF)](https://nazra.org/sites/nazra/files/attachments/questions_and_answers_engineers_syndicate_law_en.pdf)
- Degree equivalence should not be treated as an automatic guarantee of syndicate registration. It depends on specialisation, institution and cohort — summarized in search from [Coventry/TKH Egypt](https://tkh.edu.eg/coventry-degree-equivalency-egypt/) and [UPEI Cairo press release](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/egyptian-engineers-syndicate-enlists-upei-cairo-campus-among-accredited-engineering-programs-idq8xm06)
- The SCU equivalence process is said to take 4–8 weeks after a complete file, and attestation (MFA/embassy) comes first. This is a secondary guide — [studyineg.com](https://studyineg.com/en/blog/certificate-equivalency-egypt-international-students)
- Equivalence compares academic level, content and **duration of study** (an Arabic advisory site, not official) — [al-ain.com](https://al-ain.com/article/mo3adlat-shahadat-khareg-masr-rules)

### Inferences
- An Egyptian BSc Engineering is 5 years (prep + 4). A 3-year BEngTech or Sydney Accord BSc (EIT, Ara) is likely to be judged **not equivalent** (Preliminary estimate). A 4-year Australian BEng (Hons) (Washington Accord) has a better chance, but this is Unknown – requires written confirmation from the SCU equivalence office.
- A transfer student whose final degree comes mostly from credit earned at NGU may face scrutiny over the residence and duration abroad (Unknown – requires written confirmation from the SCU).

### Gaps
- Official SCU (scu.eg) equivalence rules for foreign engineering bachelors, including minimum study years abroad, in-person attendance and online study, were not retrieved.
